CASI was the Air Force's open-source research shop on Chinese air, space and missile forces. Its civilian positions lost funding on October 1, 2026, and its director has said the institute will close once those staff leave. This page keeps a copy of its publications so they stay easy to find.

The files were collected from the Internet Archive's captures of airuniversity.af.edu/CASI. Titles and dates come from CASI's own article pages where one was archived. Otherwise the title is the original file name, and the date is the one in the file name or stored in the PDF. The PDFs are kept in a separate repository so this one stays small. Each entry links to that copy and to the Wayback Machine capture it came from. This site is not affiliated with CASI, Air University or the Department of the Air Force.
