**Bottom line:** US officials remain divided over whether a Chinese businesswoman linked to Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang acted as a Beijing intelligence asset, with the FBI inquiry still open and no public proof produced. ## Espionage and counterintelligence

**US intelligence remains split on whether Nvidia CEO's associate acted as a Chinese spy.** US officials are divided over whether Alice Huang, a Chinese businesswoman who built a relationship with Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, knowingly passed information to Beijing, and several outlets reported this week that officials have found no proof the Chinese government specifically directed her conduct. The dispute traces to a classified memo Secretary of State Marco Rubio built on a CIA report about a year ago; CIA officials weren't convinced the intelligence proved Huang was a spy, and Attorney General Todd Blanche says neither the Justice Department nor the FBI has confirmed it, with the FBI inquiry still open. *Why it matters:* a counterintelligence concern that sat unresolved for about a year before surfacing publicly shows how slowly suspected foreign access to a major US tech company's leadership can get adjudicated.
([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/10/nvidia-spy-scandal-fuels-debate-over-chip-exports-to-china), [IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/us-intelligence-divided-alice-huang-spy-allegations-1832034))

**Microsoft says Salt Typhoon and North Korean hackers are adopting an AI hacking tool.** Microsoft said Salt Typhoon, the Chinese hacking group linked to breaches of US telecom networks, is rapidly integrating the Artex AI tool, already implicated in several high-profile breaches, into its hacking suite, alongside groups tied to North Korea. *Why it matters:* state-backed hacking groups adopting shared AI tooling could make espionage campaigns faster to mount and harder to attribute.
([The Record](https://therecord.media/microsoft-china-salt-typhoon-using-artex-ai-tool))

**German prosecutors arrest two alleged members of a pro-Iranian terrorist network.** Germany's Federal Prosecutor's Office said federal police arrested Iraqi nationals Mostafa Al-R. and Ameer J. in Heidelberg and Cologne on November 26, acting on arrest warrants issued November 18 over their alleged membership in a pro-Iranian terrorist organization. *Why it matters:* the arrests are part of a broader German push against networks tied to Iran-backed groups operating on its soil.
([Bundesanwaltschaft via Presseportal](https://www.presseportal.de/blaulicht/pm/14981/6398384))

## US military and intelligence community

**Army says first hypersonic missile battery is fully fielded, after years of delays.** Lt. Gen. Frank Lozano, who oversees the Army's hypersonic missile program, said the service's first Dark Eagle long-range hypersonic missile battery has been fully fielded, though he declined to say exactly when it reached that status. The program, based at Joint Base Lewis-McChord, had aimed to finish arming the battery by the end of 2025 but slipped further behind schedule. *Why it matters:* fielding the US's first ground-launched hypersonic weapon gives the Army a long-range strike capability it has pursued for years against persistent technical setbacks.
([Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/11/first-dark-eagle-hypersonic-battery-fully-fielded-lozano-says/))

## China, Russia, Iran and North Korea

**Russia's parliament advances a record three-year defense budget.** Russia's State Duma passed in a second reading a 2027-2029 budget that sets national defense spending at roughly 17.1 trillion rubles in 2027, falling slightly to about 16.3 trillion rubles by 2029, for a three-year total near 50 trillion rubles, or roughly $600 billion. Vladimir Putin is expected to sign the budget into law in early December. *Why it matters:* Moscow is choosing to keep war spending at record levels through 2029, cutting education, health and social spending to pay for it, rather than taper off as the war grinds on.
([SIPRI](https://www.sipri.org/commentary/topical-backgrounder/2026/military-spending-russias-draft-federal-budget-2027-2029))

**North Korea pauses arms shipments to Russia, closes its airspace to Russian transport planes.** Ukraine's military intelligence agency, the Main Intelligence Directorate (HUR), says North Korea halted shipments of KN-23 ballistic missiles and artillery shells to Russia and barred Russian military transport aircraft from North Korean airspace, after Russian Deputy Foreign Minister Andrei Rudenko irritated Pyongyang by suggesting Moscow could mediate tensions on the Korean peninsula. HUR says the pause doesn't extend to North Korean troops already in Russia, and that Pyongyang is separately holding back a Russian request to expand its troop contingent from 8,500 to 50,000. *Why it matters:* a diplomatic snub serious enough to interrupt military supplies shows real friction in the Moscow-Pyongyang partnership, not unconditional alignment.
([Euromaidan Press](https://euromaidanpress.com/2026/11/26/north-korea-closes-its-skies-to-russian-military-transport-planes-and-pauses-missile-and-shell-deliveries-says-ukraines-intelligence/))

**NATO sees no Russian naval buildup in the Mediterranean after Ukrainian strike near Libya.** NATO says it hasn't detected a Russian naval buildup in the Mediterranean following a Ukrainian strike near Libya, though analysts warn the strike raises the risk of Russian retaliation and heightened tensions along NATO's southern flank. *Why it matters:* a strike that reaches into the Mediterranean theater widens the geographic scope of the Russia-Ukraine war, even without a visible naval response so far.
([Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/11/nato-sees-no-russian-naval-buildup-in-the-med-after-strike-near-libya-but-analysts-warn-of-wider-risks/))

**US tightens Nvidia chip export curbs after loophole reports; Nvidia halts shipments to China.** The Commerce Department issued a rule on November 16 requiring data centers in Singapore, Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia and Brazil to get US export licenses within 90 days if they supply significant computing power to China-headquartered firms, after reports that Chinese companies had been reaching top-tier Nvidia chips through overseas subsidiaries. Nvidia's interim CEO said separately that the company has halted shipments of its H200 chip to China, leaving roughly 600,000 chips stored in Hong Kong after Beijing withheld approval for large-scale imports. *Why it matters:* even after years of export controls, Chinese buyers kept finding routes to advanced US chips, and Washington is now trying to close the overseas-subsidiary loophole specifically.
([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/17/us-moves-to-block-chinas-remote-access-to-ai-chips-amid-nvidia-scandal))

## Germany and Israel

**Israel raises defense budget by $5 billion amid strain over future funding.** Israel increased its defense budget by $5 billion, the latest in a series of increases this year, as the government weighs the cost against other spending priorities. *Why it matters:* repeated emergency increases suggest Israel's original defense budget planning has consistently underestimated the cost of sustained operations.
([Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/11/israel-increases-defense-budget-by-5-billion-as-concerns-continue-over-future-funding/))

**Bundeswehr engineers help Poland fortify NATO's eastern border.** Around 40 Bundeswehr soldiers, mostly specialized engineer troops, have been deployed in northeast Poland since July, digging trenches, laying barbed wire and building anti-tank barriers near the border with Kaliningrad and Belarus under Poland's "East Shield" fortification program. The deployment came at Poland's invitation. *Why it matters:* German troops building physical defenses on NATO's eastern flank show Berlin backing its rhetoric about deterring Russia with actual boots on the ground.
([Deutschland.de](https://www.deutschland.de/en/news/bundeswehr-supports-poland-with-protecting-natos-eastern-border))

**Left Party lawmakers disrupt Bundestag event featuring former Mossad chief.** Left Party lawmakers blocked an entrance to a closed-door Bundestag event featuring former Mossad chief David Barnea, and a Jewish activist was forcibly removed after heckling, Haaretz reported. Barnea faces a war crimes complaint in Germany over the 2024 Lebanon pager attack, which foreign reports have attributed to Israel but which Israeli officials have never confirmed. *Why it matters:* the disruption shows an operation Israel has never officially acknowledged still carries real legal and political weight in Germany.
([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/world-news/europe/2026-11-25/ty-article/.premium/no-platform-for-war-criminals-ex-mossad-chiefs-bundestag-event-disrupted/000001a2-1285-d0d9-a3aa-3affef9b0000))

**US ambassador says military aid to Israel won't stop by year's end.** US Ambassador Mike Huckabee said military aid to Israel will not cease by the end of the year, pushing back on what he called a misunderstanding among some Americans about the assistance, and said Trump remains willing to resume military action against Iran. *Why it matters:* the ambassador's comments signal Washington isn't backing off its commitments to Israel, or ruling out renewed strikes on Iran, despite shifting Democratic support.
([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/2026-11-25/ty-article/.premium/u-s-military-aid-to-israel-will-not-stop-by-end-of-year-envoy-huckabee-says/000001a2-1306-dc94-abf2-3777a83a0000))
