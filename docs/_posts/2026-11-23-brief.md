**Bottom line:** The FBI is investigating an apparent hack of the National Crime Information Center, the database police nationwide rely on for criminal records. Israeli military intelligence has reportedly warned Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu that a unilateral strike on Iran could ignite a wider war across Lebanon, Syria and Gaza, even as Israel's Mossad chief pushed Washington for renewed joint strikes. Separately, espionage allegations tied to Nvidia's ousted CEO are expected to shadow Trump's meeting with Xi Jinping.

## Espionage and counterintelligence

**FBI investigating apparent hack of its criminal history database.** The FBI is looking into an apparent breach of the National Crime Information Center (NCIC), the database federal, state, tribal and local police use to share criminal records. The bureau has not detailed the scope of the intrusion, who is responsible, or what data was accessed. *Why it matters:* NCIC sits at the center of US law enforcement data-sharing, so even a limited compromise could ripple through police work nationwide.
([BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cvze7de86pk3o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss))

**Nvidia espionage allegations shadow Trump-Xi meeting.** Ousted Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's alleged relationship with a Chinese agent identified as Alice Huang is likely to come up when President Trump meets Chinese President Xi Jinping at the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) leaders' meeting in Shenzhen. The Cipher Brief argues the case shows how espionage now reaches into the commercial AI chip supply chain both powers depend on. *Why it matters:* the case ties a flagship US tech company directly to the export-control fight with Beijing over advanced chips.
([The Cipher Brief](https://www.thecipherbrief.com/the-nvidia-spy-allegations-loom-over-trump-s-meeting-with-xi), [The Cipher Brief](https://www.thecipherbrief.com/what-the-nvidia-scandal-reveals-about-modern-espionage))

**Denmark expels Russian defense attaché over alleged plot to kill US-Ukrainian rapper.** Denmark has accused Russia of plotting to assassinate a US-Ukrainian rapper on Danish soil and expelled Russia's defense attaché over the allegation. The Russian embassy in Copenhagen denied it. *Why it matters:* it's the latest sign Denmark treats Russian intelligence activity on its territory as an active, ongoing threat.
([BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmde1lj4yjxno?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss))

**Chinese hackers used open-source AI tool to hunt a zero-day in university attacks, Amazon says.** Amazon researchers say a China-linked threat actor used the open-source AI penetration-testing tool Cairn to persistently attack two universities, and in one case may have discovered a zero-day vulnerability. Amazon did not name the specific group behind the intrusions. *Why it matters:* it's an early example of AI tools lowering the bar for state-linked hacking campaigns against under-defended targets like research universities.
([The Record](https://therecord.media/amazon-research-cairn-ai-university-attacks-possible-zero-day))

**Uzbek professor reportedly convicted of espionage in Kazakhstan.** Dr. Ikboljon Qoraboyev has reportedly been sentenced to 10 years in prison on espionage charges in Kazakhstan, according to his relatives. Neither Kazakh nor Uzbek authorities have commented on the case. *Why it matters:* it adds to a pattern of Central Asian states using espionage charges against academics and other civil-society figures.
([The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2026/11/uzbek-professor-reportedly-convicted-of-espionage-in-kazakhstan/))

## US military and intelligence community

**NSA resumes normal operations after internal network disruptions.** The NSA says it has resumed normal operations following unspecified internal network disruptions, though some facilities remain under "Code Orange," meaning delayed openings and check-ins for non-essential employees. The agency has not said what caused the disruption. *Why it matters:* even a brief, unexplained outage at NSA draws scrutiny given the agency's role in signals intelligence and cyber defense.
([The Record](https://therecord.media/nsa-resumes-normal-operations-following-internal-disruptions))

**Pentagon CIO Davies named acting National Cyber Director.** Kirsten Davies, the Pentagon's chief information officer, is taking over as acting National Cyber Director at the White House, replacing Sean Cairncross. *Why it matters:* the National Cyber Director role coordinates cyber policy government-wide, and the change comes as the FBI and other agencies deal with fresh breach reports.
([Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/11/pentagon-cio-davies-tapped-as-acting-national-cyber-director/))

**Golden Dome missile defense program advances despite funding stopgap.** A continuing resolution isn't slowing progress on Golden Dome, the missile defense program, according to deputy director Marcia Holmes, who said the program is moving ahead on obligated fiscal 2026 reconciliation funds. *Why it matters:* it signals the administration is prioritizing Golden Dome funding even while broader defense appropriations remain stalled.
([DefenseScoop](https://defensescoop.com/2026/11/19/cr-not-slowing-golden-dome-progress-deputy-director-argues/))

## China, Russia, Iran and North Korea

**Ukraine expands naval campaign against Russian assets, sinks corvette in Libyan port.** Ukraine confirmed it was behind the sinking of the Russian corvette Merkury in the Libyan port of Benghazi over the weekend, President Volodymyr Zelensky said, releasing video of the strike. Ukraine has also struck two Russian-linked cargo ships far from the Black Sea in the same stretch of days, one in the Mediterranean and one in the Baltic. *Why it matters:* Kyiv is now reaching Russian naval and shipping assets well outside the Black Sea, widening the war's geographic reach.
([Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/global/mideast-africa/2026/11/23/ukraine-confirms-it-sank-russian-corvette-in-libyan-port/), [The War Zone](https://www.twz.com/sea/ukraines-campaign-against-russian-naval-assets-far-beyond-the-black-sea-enters-a-new-phase))

**Trump pushes Iran strike decision into next year.** President Trump said he will wait until "after the beginning of the year" to decide on further strikes on Iran, telling reporters "we've got time" after returning from the China summit. The Senate again rejected a bid to curb the administration's war powers over Iran. *Why it matters:* the delay buys Iran more time to rebuild while keeping the threat of renewed US strikes hanging over Tehran.
([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/us-news/2026-11-20/ty-article/trump-pushes-iran-strike-decision-to-next-year-weve-got-time/000001a1-f623-d175-abf5-fe3795240000))

**Israel strikes Hezbollah missile manufacturing site in Bekaa Valley for sixth time.** The Israel Defense Forces (IDF) struck what it says is Hezbollah's largest underground facility for producing and storing precision-guided missiles, near the Syrian border in Lebanon's Bekaa Valley, on November 19. It was the sixth strike on the site. *Why it matters:* repeated strikes on the same facility suggest Hezbollah keeps rebuilding missile production there despite the campaign against it.
([Alma Center](https://israel-alma.org/hezbollah-precision-guided-missile-manufacturing-site-on-syrian-border/))

**Bahrain says Iranian strikes hit industrial facility.** Bahrain, which hosts the US Navy's Fifth Fleet, said it intercepted several Iranian aerial attacks over the past week and that one strike caused a fire at an industrial facility. Gulf Arab states have faced repeated fire from Tehran since the war began in February. *Why it matters:* continued Iranian strikes on a Gulf host nation for US forces keep the war's spillover risk alive.
([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/middle-east-news/iran/2026-11-21/ty-article/bahrain-says-iranian-attack-hits-industrial-facility-causes-fire/000001a1-ffcb-d9ff-a7b5-ffeffe980000))

## Germany and Israel

**Israeli military intelligence warns Netanyahu a solo strike on Iran could spark multi-front war.** Israeli Military Intelligence (Aman) told Prime Minister Netanyahu in a closed-door meeting last week that an attack on Iran could ignite "multiple fronts," spreading into Lebanon, Syria and Gaza, according to Channel 12, as reported by the Times of Israel. That contradicts the expectation of Netanyahu and Mossad chief Roman Gofman that any operation would stay limited to three to five days. *Why it matters:* a public split between political leadership and military intelligence over the scope of a strike raises the stakes of any decision to act alone.
([The Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/idf-said-to-warn-netanyahu-an-israeli-attack-on-iran-could-spark-multi-front-war/))

**Mossad chief lobbied Washington for renewed joint strikes on Iran.** Mossad chief Roman Gofman met CIA Director John Ratcliffe and White House envoy Steve Witkoff during a Washington visit last week and pushed for renewed US-Israeli strikes on Iran, according to the Wall Street Journal. Israeli military assessments reportedly question whether Iran poses an imminent threat. *Why it matters:* a gap between Israel's intelligence chief and its own military's assessment complicates any US decision on resuming the air campaign.
([Arab News](https://www.arabnews.pk/middle-east/israeli-spy-chief-lobbied-for-joint-iran-strikes-on-us-visit-sources-say-3007445))

**Bundeswehr to start training troops on kamikaze drones this year.** Germany's armed forces plan to begin training troops on loitering munitions, commonly called kamikaze drones, before the end of the year, following successful live-fire testing of systems from manufacturers Stark and Helsing. First deliveries to units are expected soon. *Why it matters:* it's a concrete step in Germany's push to field attritable strike drones as it builds up forces on NATO's eastern flank.
([Augen geradeaus!, in German](https://augengeradeaus.net/2026/11/bundeswehr-startet-ausbildung-an-kamikaze-drohnen-noch-in-diesem-jahr/))

**US donors give Mossad $20 million for sports complex as agency budget doubles.** US Jewish philanthropists have donated $20 million to Mossad for a gym and pool, including a government-matched $8 million following an earlier $12 million donation in July 2025. Haaretz reports the combined Mossad and Shin Bet budget has more than doubled since 2022. *Why it matters:* private financing of an intelligence agency's facilities, alongside a doubling official budget, raises oversight questions about how the money is tracked.
([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/2026-11-22/ty-article/.premium/u-s-jewish-philanthropists-donate-20-million-to-mossad-for-sports-complex/000001a2-03e4-d786-a3b7-2ffe5fa50000))
