**Bottom line:** Mossad chief Roman Gofman lobbied the Trump administration in Washington this week to resume joint strikes on Iran, but the White House is holding off, with Trump saying he'll decide on any new campaign "after the beginning of the year" even as the US widened its naval blockade of Iran. Trump, Xi and Putin held their first trilateral summit in Shenzhen and issued a "peace declaration," but it produced no Ukraine ceasefire, and Russia hit Kyiv's bridges days after Ukraine struck the Crimean Bridge.

## Espionage and counterintelligence

**Mossad chief pressed Trump administration for renewed Iran strikes during Washington visit.** Mossad Director Roman Gofman met CIA Director John Ratcliffe and other Trump administration officials in Washington this week and briefed them on Israeli intelligence on Iran, pushing for the US to resume joint military action, according to Al-Monitor and Axios. A White House official said Trump believes the economic pressure campaign on Iran is already "devastating" and sees no need for new "major combat operations." *Why it matters:* it's a public sign of daylight between Jerusalem and Washington over whether to restart the Iran war. ([Al-Monitor](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/11/israeli-spy-chief-lobbied-joint-iran-strikes-us-visit-sources-say), [Axios](https://axios.com/2026/11/13/mossad-israel-iran-war-netanyahu-pressures-trump))

**Nvidia espionage allegations loom over Trump-Xi meeting.** Reports that former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang had an extramarital relationship with a Chinese national, Alice Huang, whom some allege is a Chinese intelligence asset, are expected to be a backdrop to Trump's meeting with Xi Jinping at the APEC summit in Shenzhen. *Why it matters:* the allegations feed into broader scrutiny of Nvidia's China ties as Washington debates AI chip export policy. ([The Cipher Brief](https://www.thecipherbrief.com/the-nvidia-spy-allegations-loom-over-trump-s-meeting-with-xi))

**Uzbek professor reportedly convicted of espionage in Kazakhstan.** Dr. Ikboljon Qoraboyev was sentenced to 10 years in prison on espionage charges; neither Kazakh nor Uzbek authorities have commented, and his relatives dispute the case. *Why it matters:* it's part of a broader pattern of Central Asian states bringing espionage charges against academics and dual nationals. ([The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2026/11/uzbek-professor-reportedly-convicted-of-espionage-in-kazakhstan/))

## US military and intelligence community

**NSA's Middle East intelligence hub hit by network outages.** A National Security Agency facility supporting Middle East intelligence has been disrupted by network outages that officials suspect were caused by a security update gone wrong, affecting communications with Middle East-based staff; the agency is relying on backup systems and has closed its headquarters to most employees. *Why it matters:* a disruption to Middle East intelligence support comes at a sensitive moment, with Washington and Israel weighing whether to resume strikes on Iran. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/us-news/2026-11-19/ty-article/report-nsa-hub-for-middle-east-intelligence-hit-by-network-outages/000001a1-f4b3-d175-abf5-feb72ea10000))

**Pentagon lacks an overarching plan for munitions production, watchdog warns.** A Government Accountability Office report found the Department of Defense has no plan linking munitions production and funding to actual requirements. *Why it matters:* the gap undercuts the Pentagon's ability to sustain a prolonged fight, as operations in Ukraine and the Middle East both draw down US stockpiles. ([Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/industry/techwatch/2026/11/19/pentagon-lacks-overarching-plan-for-munitions-production-watchdog-warns/))

**Golden Dome missile defense program says it isn't being slowed by the funding lapse.** Deputy director Marcia Holmes said the Golden Dome missile defense effort is still moving ahead using obligated fiscal year 2026 reconciliation dollars, despite the continuing resolution affecting other programs. *Why it matters:* it signals the administration is shielding its signature missile defense program from broader budget disruption. ([DefenseScoop](https://defensescoop.com/2026/11/19/cr-not-slowing-golden-dome-progress-deputy-director-argues/))

**Navy likely to field drone wingmen before its next-generation fighter.** William Toti, who has served as acting Navy undersecretary since May and is now up for permanent appointment, told lawmakers the Navy will likely fly Collaborative Combat Aircraft drones before the F/A-XX fighter enters service. *Why it matters:* it suggests the Navy is leaning on uncrewed systems to bridge a capability gap while its next crewed fighter program slips. ([Defense One](https://www.defenseone.com/defense-systems/2026/11/navy-will-likely-fly-ccas-sooner-f-xx-white-house-nom-says/417365/))

## China, Russia, Iran and North Korea

**Trump, Xi and Putin hold first trilateral summit, issue "peace declaration."** The three leaders met in Shenzhen for their first trilateral talks, covering Ukraine, nuclear arms control and UN reform, and issued a joint "peace declaration." Putin secured a propaganda win without offering concessions on Ukraine, and the summit produced few tangible results otherwise, analysts said. *Why it matters:* the optics of the meeting may outlast its substance, with no ceasefire or arms-control breakthrough attached to it. ([Foreign Policy](https://foreignpolicy.com/2026/11/19/trump-xi-putin-trilateral-talks-nuclear-disarmament-un-icc-oil/), [Atlantic Council](https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/dispatches/putin-didnt-get-his-yalta-but-hes-happy-nonetheless/), [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c623j4ln97x7o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss))

**Russia hit Kyiv's bridges days after Ukraine struck the Crimean Bridge.** A wave of Russian missile strikes hit three bridges in Kyiv, killing five people, after Ukraine's attack on the Crimean Bridge earlier in the week; Putin had vowed a "far more painful" response to that strike. *Why it matters:* both sides are escalating attacks on transport infrastructure, raising the risk of further civilian casualties even as peace-talk rhetoric continues elsewhere. ([BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6x29gn4pdn9o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss), [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cm879dqn3e1jo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss))

**Trump pushes Iran strike decision to next year as US expands naval blockade.** Trump said he would decide on any new round of strikes on Iran "after the beginning of the year," after the Senate again rejected a bid to curb the administration's war powers; separately, the US expanded its naval blockade of Iranian ports to cover any ship receiving cargo via ship-to-ship transfer from an Iranian-loaded vessel. *Why it matters:* the blockade expansion shows Washington is tightening economic pressure on Iran even while holding off on new strikes. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/us-news/2026-11-20/ty-article/trump-pushes-iran-strike-decision-to-next-year-weve-got-time/000001a1-f623-d175-abf5-fe3795240000), [USNI News](https://news.usni.org/2026/11/19/u-s-expands-iranian-blockade-as-ship-attacks-continue))

**Russia and Iran expand cooperation.** Russia and Iran have broadened and accelerated cooperation across nuclear energy and political affairs; Rosatom's director general announced new nuclear cooperation in late October. *Why it matters:* deepening Russia-Iran ties complicate US and Israeli efforts to isolate Tehran. ([Jamestown Foundation](https://jamestown.org/russia-and-iran-seek-closer-cooperation/))

## Germany and Israel

**War crimes complaint filed against ex-Mossad chief ahead of Germany visit.** The European Center for Constitutional and Human Rights filed a complaint against former Mossad chief David Barnea under Germany's universal jurisdiction laws over the Lebanon pager attacks, citing his own past remarks as grounds to suspect responsibility; Barnea is set to speak at a pro-Israel event in Germany's parliament next week. *Why it matters:* it's a test of whether German courts will act on universal jurisdiction claims against a senior Israeli official. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/israel-politics/2026-11-19/ty-article/.premium/war-crimes-complaint-filed-against-ex-mossad-chief-ahead-of-germany-visit/000001a1-f378-dad7-a9fd-f37b78dd0000))

**Bundeswehr to start training troops on kamikaze drones this year.** Germany's military plans to begin training troops on loitering munitions, commonly called kamikaze drones, before the end of the year, following successful live-fire testing of systems from manufacturers Stark and Helsing. *Why it matters:* it's a concrete step in the Bundeswehr's push to field strike drones at scale. ([Augen geradeaus!, in German](https://augengeradeaus.net/2026/11/bundeswehr-startet-ausbildung-an-kamikaze-drohnen-noch-in-diesem-jahr/))

**Israel strikes Hezbollah stronghold deep in Lebanon's Bekaa region.** The Israeli military struck the Bekaa region, a Hezbollah stronghold about 50 miles beyond the IDF-occupied buffer zone in southern Lebanon, for the first time in months. *Why it matters:* it shows Israel is willing to strike well beyond the buffer zone it maintains under the Lebanon ceasefire. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/middle-east-news/lebanonnews/2026-11-19/ty-article/.premium/israel-strikes-lebanons-bekaa-region-for-first-time-in-months/000001a1-f4f4-dad7-a9fd-f6ffce620000))
