**Bottom line:** Israelis voted today with Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's push for a pre-election strike on Iran still unresolved, after Haaretz reported Mossad's chief broke with the military chief over the plan's timing. ## Espionage and counterintelligence

**James Comey was indicted a third time, now under the Espionage Act.** Prosecutors allege the disclosure of classified information to the New York Times in 2016, according to Comey's lawyers and the Justice Department; his earlier indictment on false-statement and obstruction charges, tied to the same Clinton email-probe leak allegations, was thrown out over how the case was brought. *Why it matters:* A third attempt to charge a former FBI director shows the Justice Department still treating the 2016 leak allegations as a live prosecution target, not a closed matter.
([BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ckly0xqjnlwvo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss))

**An Israeli lawyer was charged with contact with a foreign agent.** The Shin Bet security service and police arrested the lawyer for questioning, and the state prosecutor is seeking to extend her detention until proceedings end, according to Haaretz. *Why it matters:* A lawyer facing a foreign-agent charge points to an active Shin Bet counterintelligence case reaching into Israel's legal profession.
([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/israel-security/2026-10-26/ty-article/.premium/israeli-lawyer-charged-with-contact-with-foreign-agent-obstruction-of-justice/000001a1-78a7-db43-a1a3-febf2a7c0000))

**Ukraine warned hackers are exploiting Chinese-made Dahua surveillance cameras.** Ukraine's CERT-UA says it identified cases where hackers compromised Dahua internet-connected cameras and video recorders, changed their settings and created unauthorized user accounts. *Why it matters:* Widely deployed Chinese-made surveillance hardware is a soft target for turning civilian infrastructure into a collection platform.
([The Record](https://therecord.media/ukraine-cert-ua-warning-chinese-dahua-cameras-hacked))

## US military and intelligence community

**Senators say the Trump administration is breaking the law by withholding a surveillance court opinion.** Section 702 of the Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act (FISA) has lapsed, but CyberScoop reports a decision on its future is near, and senators and rights groups argue a classified Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Court opinion the administration is withholding could be decisive. *Why it matters:* Keeping the court's own reasoning secret limits Congress's ability to judge whether a surveillance power should be revived.
([CyberScoop](https://cyberscoop.com/wyden-schiff-fisa-section-702-declassification/))

**The Pentagon has built a covert office to sabotage foreign technology programs, Politico reported.** According to Politico's reporting, the Department of Defense created the Technology Support Office this year under the undersecretary for intelligence and security, tasked with disrupting foreign projects, especially in artificial intelligence and unmanned systems, through tools like supply-chain interference and deception operations; a Pentagon spokesperson disputed the account, and the office has not been officially announced. *Why it matters:* If accurate, it marks a new, deliberately low-visibility arm of US offensive technology competition with adversaries, with little disclosed oversight.
([TechRadar](https://techradar.com/pro/security/the-pentagon-has-a-covert-tech-sabotage-agency-that-they-really-dont-want-you-to-know-about-and-there-are-big-questions-about-its-oversight))

**The Navy picked its first submarine for Australia's AUKUS rotational force.** The Pentagon announced the Virginia-class USS Colorado (SSN-788) will be the lead nuclear attack submarine assigned to Submarine Rotational Force-West, based at HMAS Stirling near Perth starting in 2027. *Why it matters:* It's a concrete step in the AUKUS (Australia-UK-US) submarine pact, which is meant to give the US and its allies a persistent undersea presence in the Indo-Pacific.
([USNI News](https://news.usni.org/2026/10/26/u-s-navy-taps-attack-boat-for-2027-australian-aukus-assignment), [Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/naval/2026/10/26/pentagon-announces-first-us-submarine-joining-submarine-rotational-force-west/))

## China, Russia, Iran and North Korea

**A UK parliamentary report says the Royal Navy lacks the ships to defend the High North from Russia.** The bipartisan House of Commons Defence Committee's "Defence in the High North" report says the Royal Navy is plagued by shortfalls in the ships and capabilities needed to counter Russia in the region. *Why it matters:* A close NATO ally publicly admitting a capability gap against Russia in a strategically important maritime zone is a gap allies, not just Britain, will have to cover.
([USNI News](https://news.usni.org/2026/10/26/royal-navy-lacks-ships-capabilities-to-defend-high-north-from-russia-says-u-k-committee-report))

**Russia's drone production surge depends on Chinese supplies, documents show.** Jamestown Foundation reports that documents obtained by Ukraine's Security and Cooperation Center and shared with Politico show Jilin Chemical Fiber Group, a Chinese state-owned company, shipped 46 metric tons of a key drone-fiber precursor to Alabuga-Volokno, a subsidiary of Russia's main drone manufacturer. *Why it matters:* It's concrete evidence that Russia's drone war against Ukraine still runs on Chinese industrial inputs, not domestic production alone.
([Jamestown Foundation](https://jamestown.org/russias-drone-surge-depends-on-chinese-supplies/))

**Pakistan, Saudi Arabia and Turkey held last-ditch talks with Iran to head off renewed fighting.** The War Zone reports the meeting came as the United States and Israel prepare for a possible new round of strikes; Haaretz reports it was the first high-level contact between Iran and the three nations, which signed a mutual defense pact in August, since the war that began with US and Israeli strikes on Iran spread to Saudi Arabia and Yemen. *Why it matters:* Regional powers are trying to head off a resumption of a war that already spread beyond Iran once.
([The War Zone](https://www.twz.com/news-features/last-ditch-meeting-in-iran-seeks-to-stave-off-a-restart-of-all-out-fighting-in-the-region), [Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/middle-east-news/2026-10-26/ty-article/saudi-arabia-turkey-and-pakistan-hold-de-escalation-talks-with-iran/000001a1-789f-dfb9-a5a9-fabfed900000))

**A new Philippine Coast Guard vessel shadowed a Chinese carrier strike group off Mindanao.** USNI News reports the Taiwan-built patrol vessel BRP Sarangani tracked the Chinese aircraft carrier Liaoning and its destroyer escorts near Mindanao. *Why it matters:* It's a small but direct sign the Philippines is willing to tail Chinese carrier groups in its own waters rather than just monitor them from a distance.
([USNI News](https://news.usni.org/2026/10/26/new-philippine-coast-guard-vessel-shadows-chinese-carrier-strike-group-off-mindanao))

## Germany and Israel

**Israel's Mossad chief backed a pre-election Iran operation that the military chief opposed, as Israelis voted today.** Haaretz reports senior Mossad officials raised concerns that the timing of a possible operation against Iran could be shaped by political considerations, with the agency's own chief sparring with aides over his support for Netanyahu's push to strike before the election; separately, security officials told Haaretz the clash with military chief Eyal Zamir is being used by Netanyahu to frame the Israel Defense Forces (IDF) as defeatist during the campaign. *Why it matters:* A public rift between Israel's political, military and intelligence leadership over a potential war, breaking out on election day itself, is a strategic risk in its own right.
([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/israel-security/2026-10-26/ty-article/.premium/mossad-officials-clash-with-spy-agency-chief-over-pre-election-operation-in-iran/000001a1-791a-db0e-a7e7-ffdb61fb0000), [Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/elections/2026-10-27/ty-article/.premium/military-officials-say-netanyahu-using-clash-with-idf-chief-for-campaign/000001a1-79d2-dfb9-a5a9-fbf705f70000))

**German intelligence and military leaders warned of a Russian "shadow war" and raised the Bundeswehr's sabotage threat level.** Federal Intelligence Service (BND) President Martin Jäger told parliament's oversight panel that Germany risks being drawn into violent conflict with Russia, pointing to a foiled drone attack on Ukrainian cargo planes in Leipzig, and warned the risk of attacks on German soil remains real; separately, an internal Bundeswehr assessment reported by WDR and Süddeutsche Zeitung raised the sabotage threat from Russia to its second-highest level for the first time, after a drone carrying explosives crashed near the Wunstorf air base in September. *Why it matters:* Germany's top intelligence and military assessments now agree that Russian sabotage against German forces and infrastructure, not just Ukraine, is an active rather than hypothetical risk.
([Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/world/german-spy-chief-warns-of-violent-conflict-risk-with-russia/4078248), [Abendzeitung München, in German](https://www.abendzeitung-muenchen.de/politik/bundeswehr-stuft-bedrohung-durch-sabotage-hoch-art-1167375))

**Cyprus will buy a new offshore patrol vessel from Israel.** Breaking Defense reports Israel Shipyards will build the vessel, which Israel's Ministry of Defense says will support expanded patrol, surveillance and search-and-rescue missions. *Why it matters:* It's a small but telling sign of how far Israel's defense-export reach extends into the Eastern Mediterranean.
([Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/10/cyprus-to-acquire-new-offshore-patrol-vessel-with-advanced-naval-systems-from-israel/))
