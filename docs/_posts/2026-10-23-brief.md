## Espionage and counterintelligence

**Germany jails Ukrainian man accused of spying for Russia; Merz says the country is a top espionage target.** Germany's federal prosecutor said the investigating judge of the Federal Court of Justice put into effect an arrest warrant against a Ukrainian national, identified as Vadym Y., who was detained a day earlier on suspicion of working as an intelligence agent. Chancellor Merz said after the arrest that Germany is the target of "intensive" espionage. *Why it matters:* It's the latest in a string of Russia-linked espionage arrests in Germany this year, and comes from the country's own leadership rather than a one-off prosecutor's statement.
([Federal Prosecutor (GBA), in German](https://www.presseportal.de/blaulicht/pm/14981/6376698), [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmx28y5e04ggo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss))

**Leaked WhatsApp messages allege a senior Belgian spy helped bury Russia-tie claims against ex-EU Commissioner Reynders.** EUobserver reported it had obtained leaked WhatsApp exchanges between former EU Commissioner Didier Reynders and a senior Belgian intelligence official that it said show attempts to bury allegations about Reynders' possible ties to Russia. Neither Reynders nor the Belgian security service has publicly responded to the specific leak. *Why it matters:* If accurate, it would point to an EU-level intelligence failure to pursue a Russia-linked conflict-of-interest case involving one of its own former commissioners.
([EUobserver](https://euobserver.com/243287/exclusive-leaked-whatsapps-between-ex-eu-commissioner-reynders-and-senior-belgian-spy-reveal-attempts-to-bury-allegations-of-his-possible-russia-ties/))

**Western diplomats describe "constant paranoia" working in wartime Moscow.** The BBC reported Western diplomats say working in Russia since its full-scale invasion of Ukraine now feels like a return to Cold War-era surveillance and suspicion. *Why it matters:* It's a sign of how far Russia's counterintelligence posture toward Western missions has hardened since 2022.
([BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cj75p1xr7p7vo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss))

## US military and intelligence community

**US Air Force veteran freed from Russian prison after Trump-Putin talks.** Stephen Hubbard, a retired teacher and US Air Force veteran, was released from Russian custody after more than four years in prison, following negotiations between Trump and Putin. *Why it matters:* Prisoner releases like this are one of the few concrete results to come out of the broader US-Russia diplomatic track.
([Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/news/your-military/2026/10/22/trump-putin-negotiate-release-of-us-air-force-veteran-from-russian-prison/))

**Pentagon and Lockheed agree to 10-year framework for 850 F-35 jets.** The Pentagon and Lockheed Martin agreed to a decade-long framework guaranteeing at least 850 F-35 Lightning II fighters for the US military, though it still needs congressional approval and funding. *Why it matters:* Locking in production at this scale shapes US and allied airpower capacity well into the 2030s.
([Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/news/pentagon-congress/2026/10/22/pentagon-lockheed-agree-to-10-year-deal-for-850-f-35-jets/))

**Army tests drone-mounted cyber and electronic warfare tools in new cyber battalion.** An Army unit is experimenting with small drones carrying "tailored cyber effects" and electronic warfare capabilities as it works out standard equipment for its 11th Cyber Battalion and a planned second battalion. *Why it matters:* It shows the Army pushing cyber and electronic warfare capability down to tactical units rather than keeping it centralized.
([Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/10/army-unit-experimenting-with-small-drones-for-tailored-cyber-effects/))

## China, Russia, Iran and North Korea

([The War Zone](https://www.twz.com/news-features/construction-of-irans-eagle-44-underground-airbase-hasnt-stopped-despite-war))

**Chinese factory images show KJ-3000 radar jets moving toward serial production.** New images from a Chinese factory show four KJ-3000 airborne early warning and control jets, suggesting China is moving the aircraft, its most capable in that class, into serial production. *Why it matters:* A larger fleet of advanced AEW&C aircraft would improve China's ability to track and coordinate against US and allied forces in a Pacific contingency.

## Germany and Israel

**Bundeswehr rates Russian sabotage threat "significant" for first time after drone found near air base.** Germany's military rated the threat of Russian sabotage as "significant," the second-highest level on its internal scale, for the first time, according to an internal assessment obtained by German broadcaster WDR and Süddeutsche Zeitung and confirmed by a defense ministry spokesperson. The reassessment followed the discovery in September of a crashed drone carrying explosives near Wunstorf air base, resembling one used in a failed August attack on cargo planes at Leipzig/Halle airport that Germany has blamed on Russia; Moscow denies involvement. The document says potential targets are no longer limited to specific regions or bases, and the military expects the situation could worsen. *Why it matters:* It marks the Bundeswehr formally treating weaponized drone attacks on its own forces, not just Ukraine-linked training sites, as a live threat.
([Reuters via Internazionale](https://www.internazionale.it/ultime-notizie-reuters/2026/10/21/germany-s-military-warns-of-significant-russian-sabotage-threat))

**Former senior Israeli military and intelligence officials accuse Netanyahu of near treason over German submarine deal.** SpyTalk reported that a group of former senior Israeli military and intelligence officials has compiled a dossier accusing Prime Minister Netanyahu of deep corruption and coverups connected to Israel's submarine purchases from Germany, with some of the allegations amounting to what the group calls near treason. *Why it matters:* Accusations of this severity from former military and intelligence leadership, rather than political rivals, add weight to the long-running submarine affair.
([SpyTalk](https://www.spytalk.co/p/top-former-israeli-military-and-intelligence))

**Haaretz: Israel's election outcome could decide timing of a new war with Iran.** Haaretz reports that the result of Tuesday's election could decisively affect whether and when Israel renews the war with Iran, though the army is not yet convinced regime change would be achievable even with a new round of fighting, and Trump's own position could still shift. *Why it matters:* It shows the Israeli military taking a more cautious line on regime change than the political directive Netanyahu has now given Mossad and the IDF.
([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/israel-security/2026-10-23/ty-article/.premium/will-post-election-uncertainty-push-netanyahu-toward-a-new-iran-confrontation/000001a1-64fd-d29a-a1a5-e6ffa3150000))

**Bundeswehr active troop count edges down again, stays within legal target range.** Germany's Bundeswehr reported its number of active soldiers fell slightly again in September to 186,200, remaining within the growth corridor set by Germany's Soldier Act. *Why it matters:* The slow pace of growth underscores the gap between Germany's stated ambitions to expand its armed forces and the recruiting reality.
([Augen geradeaus!, in German](https://augengeradeaus.net/2026/10/personalstaerke-september-2026-erneuter-rueckgang-mit-186-200-im-zielkorridor-neufassung/))
