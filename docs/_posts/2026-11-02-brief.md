**Bottom line:** The FBI formally attributed last week's record $6.3 billion Binance hack to North Korean state hackers, though Trump is publicly questioning that call. Washington is weighing whether to resume strikes on Iran as early as the day after Tuesday's US midterms, with Netanyahu pushing for action and Tehran warning of a longer war than before. Separately, the New York Times reported that a Chinese data-center executive cultivated a close relationship with Nvidia's CEO as part of a Beijing intelligence effort, an account Nvidia calls a mischaracterization of routine business contact.

## Espionage and counterintelligence

**The FBI blamed North Korea's TraderTraitor group for the biggest crypto heist on record, and Trump is pushing back on that call.** The FBI and US Secret Service said North Korean state hackers stole more than $6.3 billion from the Binance exchange and urged the crypto industry to block transactions tied to the theft. Trump has since questioned the bureau's attribution. *Why it matters:* The theft, the largest cryptocurrency hack ever recorded, shows North Korea's cyber theft operations are funding the regime at a bigger scale than before. ([The Record](https://therecord.media/fbi-blames-tradertraitor-for-binance-hack), [Foreign Policy](https://foreignpolicy.com/2026/10/30/binance-cryptocurrency-hack-north-korea/))

**Nvidia's CEO was reportedly the unwitting target of a Chinese intelligence-gathering effort.** The New York Times reported that US officials concluded a Chinese executive at Megaspeed, a Singapore-based data-center firm already under scrutiny for allegedly diverting restricted Nvidia chips to China, cultivated a close relationship with Jensen Huang to pass information to Beijing. Officials reportedly believe Huang was unaware of her activity; Trump was briefed and took no action. Singapore questioned the executive and released her. Nvidia called the contact routine, saying Huang talks with thousands of customer executives a year, and Beijing called the report "groundless speculation." *Why it matters:* The allegation, unconfirmed and based on anonymous sources, nonetheless underscores how Chinese intelligence services are said to be targeting executives with access to export-controlled AI chip technology. ([The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/aseanplus/aseanplus-news/2026/10/30/china-dismisses-us-report-of-suspected-spy-targeting-nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-as-groundless), [ABC7 News](https://abc7news.com/story/nvidia-denies-report-ceo-was-allegedly-targeted-chinese-spy-plot/20000206/))

**A US defense contractor pleaded guilty to helping North Korea's remote-IT-worker fraud scheme.** A 62-year-old contractor admitted letting co-conspirators use his identity and his top-secret security clearance to apply for almost 100 jobs last year, according to CyberScoop; the scheme had limited success. *Why it matters:* The case shows North Korea's remote-worker fraud scheme, which helps fund its weapons programs, keeps targeting cleared US contractors despite years of FBI warnings. ([CyberScoop](https://cyberscoop.com/defense-contractor-guilty-north-korea-scheme-facilitator/))

**China-linked hackers used an AI-driven pentesting tool to breach 140 targets in 11 days.** CrowdStrike said a group it tracks as Vapor Panda used an agentic AI penetration-testing tool to compromise more than 200 organizations across at least 14 countries, calling the campaign remarkably successful and fast. *Why it matters:* The speed and scale show AI tools are letting state-linked hacking groups operate faster than defenders can respond. ([CyberScoop](https://cyberscoop.com/china-vapor-panda-agentic-ai-artex-campaign-crowdstrike/))

## US military and intelligence community

**The Pentagon disclosed its FY2026 intelligence budgets.** The Military Intelligence Program, which funds Pentagon-run intelligence activities, came in at $30.9 billion, while the separate National Intelligence Program, which covers agencies like the CIA, totaled $78.1 billion, Breaking Defense reported. *Why it matters:* The disclosure, required by law, is one of the few public data points on the overall size of US intelligence spending. ([Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/10/pentagon-reveals-fy26-intelligence-budget-at-30-9-billion/))

**The Pentagon has asked NATO allies to tone down their rhetoric on Russia.** Defense News reported, citing sources, that Pentagon officials have in recent weeks asked several NATO countries to dial back public statements about the threat Russia poses. *Why it matters:* The request suggests Washington wants to avoid rhetoric that could complicate its own diplomacy with Moscow over Ukraine. ([Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/global/europe/2026/10/30/pentagon-tells-europeans-to-cool-their-rhetoric-on-russia-sources-say/))

## China, Russia, Iran and North Korea

**Trump is weighing a resumption of Iran strikes right after the US midterms, as Netanyahu pushes for action.** Reports say Washington is preparing to resume strikes on Iran as soon as November 4, the day after the midterm elections. Iran's Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei met with military commanders and ordered them to update the target list of US and Israeli assets, and Iran's army said any new war could last longer than the last one. Trump and Netanyahu reportedly remain divided on timing, with Trump saying he wants to resolve the standoff "peacefully" if he can. *Why it matters:* A resumption of strikes this soon after the midterms would reopen a conflict that had appeared to be cooling, with Washington and Jerusalem reportedly still not aligned on the goal. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/middle-east-news/2026-10-31/ty-article/irans-khamenei-meets-military-brass-as-u-s-said-to-plot-post-midterm-strikes/000001a1-915e-def7-adad-9bfe31080001), [Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/us-news/2026-11-02/ty-article/.premium/trump-says-he-had-very-good-talk-with-netanyahu-discussed-iran/000001a1-9a32-de48-abb1-dff3dc890000), [SpyTalk](https://www.spytalk.co/p/spyweek-trump-weighs-new-iran-war))

**Iran used a limpet mine in its latest Strait of Hormuz attack, a new report says.** The War Zone reported that Iran placed a limpet mine on a tanker's hull in its most recent attack in the strait, as the US continues moving more air defenses, ships, troops and supplies into the Middle East. *Why it matters:* The attack method and the US buildup both point toward preparations for a wider air campaign against Iran if talks fail. ([The War Zone](https://www.twz.com/news-features/iran-used-limpet-mine-placed-on-ships-hull-in-latest-strait-of-hormuz-attack-report))

**North Korea showed off a growing drone arsenal.** Pyongyang displayed a range of new reconnaissance and attack drones at a recent event, including a stealthy uncrewed combat aircraft concept it is aspiring to build. *Why it matters:* The lineup shows North Korea drawing the same drone-warfare lessons from Russia's war in Ukraine that its neighbors are. ([The War Zone](https://www.twz.com/air/highlights-from-north-koreas-drone-a-palooza))

**Russia and North Korea tested ballistic missiles in the Western Pacific in the same period.** Russia's defense ministry said it launched a submarine-launched ballistic missile from the Western Pacific toward its Arctic region, while North Korea carried out its own missile launches around the same time, USNI News reported. *Why it matters:* The simultaneous tests point to deepening coordination between the two militaries. ([USNI News](https://news.usni.org/2026/10/30/russia-north-korea-test-missiles-in-the-western-pacific))

## Germany and Israel

**Germany's new combat helicopters will initially be unable to fire their weapons, the federal audit office warns.** Germany's Bundesrechnungshof raised concerns about the Bundeswehr's purchase of H145M light combat helicopters, questioning whether the aircraft can adequately replace the Tiger attack helicopter, particularly on crew protection, and flagging that it still lacks the military certification needed to carry weapons. The audit office also criticized the financing, saying 714 million euros would have to come from the regular defense budget rather than the special fund. *Why it matters:* It is the latest in a run of procurement setbacks for the Bundeswehr's rearmament push. ([echo24.de, in German](https://www.echo24.de/welt/maengel-pistorius-bundeswehr-kampfhubschrauber-deal-helikopter-airbus-rechnungshof-druck-zr-92725390.html), [leinetal24.de, in German](https://www.leinetal24.de/welt/maengel-pistorius-bundeswehr-kampfhubschrauber-deal-helikopter-airbus-rechnungshof-druck-zr-92725390.html))

**Mossad is joining Israel's election-interference probe as it expands beyond Qatar, while a Haaretz investigation says the underlying claims were overblown.** The Shin Bet's inquiry, opened at the Central Elections Committee's request over "serious suspicions" of foreign interference, is now being widened with Mossad's help to look at countries beyond Qatar. A separate Haaretz investigation found the original bribery allegations and claims of Qatari influence were "deliberately blown out of proportion," while the committee's acting director general said he knew of no evidence to support them. *Why it matters:* Critics say Prime Minister Netanyahu has used the Shin Bet and the interference claims to undermine confidence in a vote he could lose. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/elections/2026-10-31/ty-article/.premium/mossad-to-help-probe-suspected-election-interference-by-countries-beyond-qatar/000001a1-8e44-d1ca-a5b7-bf74bf350000), [Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/elections/2026-11-01/ty-article/.premium/how-israels-shin-bet-and-police-joined-netanyahus-bid-to-undermine-the-vote/000001a1-9593-d56e-a7a1-95f3c9c30000))

**The Israel Defense Forces and Shin Bet reported mixed results in West Bank and Gaza operations.** The military said a 10-day raid in Hebron, which displaced roughly 3,300 Palestinians and destroyed the homes of two men accused of killing two Israeli soldiers, turned up just four weapons. Separately, Israel Defense Forces officials assess Hamas has rebuilt its weapons arsenal in Gaza to hundreds of rockets, aided by smuggling, and are urging an end to the West Bank operation. *Why it matters:* The small weapons haul in Hebron, set against the Hamas rearmament assessment, raises questions about whether the West Bank operation is meeting its stated security goal. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/west-bank/2026-11-02/ty-article/.premium/idf-says-it-seized-just-4-weapons-in-major-10-day-raid-in-west-banks-hebron/000001a1-9b87-d1dd-abbd-dbdfe7c20000), [Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/israel-security/2026-11-02/ty-article/.premium/idf-warns-hamas-rebuilding-weapons-production-urges-end-to-west-bank-operation/000001a1-98a9-dc07-a3f5-dabb61bf0000))
