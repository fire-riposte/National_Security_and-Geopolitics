## Espionage and counterintelligence

**Trump confirms FBI is investigating Nvidia CEO's alleged Chinese spy affair.** President Trump said the FBI is looking into Jensen Huang's affair with Alice Huang (no relation), a Singapore-based executive US intelligence has assessed as a Chinese intelligence asset, after reports tied the relationship to an estimated $7.5 billion in Nvidia chip shipments to China. Trump has publicly defended Huang, while Democrats in Congress are pushing for a fuller investigation of Nvidia's export compliance. *Why it matters:* a chip executive's personal case now sits at the center of the fight over how much AI computing power the US lets China buy. ([BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqg7x7dpvym8o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss), [Foreign Policy](https://foreignpolicy.com/2026/11/09/jensen-huang-spy-scandal-alice-nvidia-trump-china/), [i](https://inews.co.uk/news/nvidia-suspected-chinese-spy-4883329))

**Russian-linked Turla group used new toolset to raid Kazakh and Romanian government email.** Cybersecurity firm ESET said the Russian state-linked hacking group Turla deployed a new toolset, nicknamed "Turla's Cereal," to quietly pull email from Microsoft Exchange servers at government agencies in Kazakhstan and Romania, built to leave few of the usual signs of compromise. *Why it matters:* it's a reminder Russian intelligence is still running long-term espionage operations against former Soviet-space and NATO-adjacent governments even while fighting in Ukraine. ([CyberScoop](https://cyberscoop.com/russian-turla-group-used-new-toolset-to-silently-pilfer-emails-from-kazakhstan-romanian-governments/))

## US military and intelligence community

**Pentagon watchdog finds thousands of F-35 parts missing.** A Government Accountability Office (GAO) review found more than 17,000 F-35 parts, worth an estimated $1.3 billion, missing from the program's inventory system. *Why it matters:* poor parts tracking on the Pentagon's most expensive weapons program raises readiness and cost-overrun risks as the services lean harder on the jet. ([Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/11/pentagon-cant-track-thousands-of-f-35-parts-gao/))

**Army pushes attack drones to nearly all its divisions.** The Army has fielded "launched effects," expendable attack and reconnaissance drones, to 11 of its 12 divisions, with Griffon Aerospace supplying most of them, under Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth's 2025 drone-dominance directive. *Why it matters:* it's one of the clearest signs the Army is restructuring how divisions fight around cheap, disposable drones rather than relying only on traditional artillery and aircraft. ([Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/11/army-fields-launched-effects-to-11-of-12-divisions-with-griffon-aerospace-delivering-the-majority/))

## China, Russia, Iran and North Korea

**France blames Iran for cyberattack that spoiled food in hundreds of supermarkets.** French authorities said hackers tied to Shahid Kaveh, a cyber unit of Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC), breached industrial control systems running refrigeration at roughly 700 supermarkets, spoiling chilled and frozen food and in some cases starting fires, and warned Tehran it could face new sanctions. Shahid Kaveh has previously been linked to attacks on US water-utility control systems. *Why it matters:* it's the first time France has formally attributed an attack on its civilian infrastructure to Iran, adding Paris to the list of Western governments accusing Tehran of sabotage rather than just espionage. ([BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmg474vk6dyqo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss), [The Record](https://therecord.media/france-blames-iran-for-fridge-cyberattacks))

**Trump plays down chance of new US strikes on Iran, even as sanctions tighten.** Trump said further American military action against Iran is "highly unlikely" for now while hosting a Qatari mediator to discuss ending the broader Iran war, and separately rolled out new sanctions he says will test whether US allies across Asia will help enforce them. *Why it matters:* the administration is leaning on sanctions and diplomacy for the moment, but strikes remain on the table if talks fail. ([BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq5yny4nl2d3o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss), [Foreign Policy](https://foreignpolicy.com/2026/11/09/trump-qatar-thani-iran-war-nvidia-huang-affair-chinese-spy/), [Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/middle-east-news/iran/2026-11-10/ty-article/.premium/trumps-economic-chokehold-on-iran-puts-u-s-allies-across-asia-to-the-test/000001a1-c166-db7d-a5e3-e57f570b0000))

**British firm converts fishing trawler into drone sub-hunter for the Arctic.** A British company is converting a 56-meter commercial fishing trawler into an uncrewed "Arctic Hunter" drone ship with 60-day endurance, meant to give the Royal Navy persistent coverage against Russian submarines in the high north. *Why it matters:* it's a cheap way to extend NATO's underwater surveillance in the Arctic, where Russian submarine activity has been rising. ([Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/global/europe/2026/11/09/firm-bets-on-converted-fishing-trawlers-to-tackle-russian-submarines-in-the-arctic/), [TWZ](https://www.twz.com/sea/fishing-trawler-adapted-into-arctic-hunter-drone-ship-aims-to-scour-for-russian-submarines))

**Ukraine, Russia and the US hold first trilateral peace talks since February.** Delegations from Kyiv, Washington and Moscow met in the United Arab Emirates for the first three-way Ukraine talks in months, with US officials describing "some flexibility" on both sides. *Why it matters:* real movement toward ending the war would reshape Europe's security picture, but previous rounds of talks have stalled without a deal. ([BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqpvgvrpjvv8o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss))

## Germany and Israel

**Israeli attorney general finds no evidence foreign interference changed election result, as feud with Shin Bet spills into public.** Attorney General Gali Baharav-Miara said investigators found no evidence foreign interference could have changed the outcome of Israel's recent election, directly at odds with Shin Bet chief David Zini, who had warned of extensive foreign financing and influence efforts without presenting evidence. Israel Police separately said they found no investigative or intelligence basis for Likud's claims that Arab-led parties "stole" the vote. *Why it matters:* an unusually public clash between the attorney general and the domestic security service's director raises questions about how Shin Bet is handling a politically charged claim. ([ynetnews](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/sjajbe06ge), [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-913293))

**Israeli officials weigh whether Iran could mount a Ukraine-style drone attack from inside Israel.** Israeli security officials are studying whether Iran could attempt an attack resembling Ukraine's 2023 Operation Spiderweb, in which smuggled drones launched from inside Russia struck Russian air bases, and Prime Minister Netanyahu has warned of a similar threat originating from within Israel. Officials quoted said the country is not fully prepared for that scenario. *Why it matters:* it shows Israeli planners treating a low-cost, hard-to-detect drone attack as a live threat rather than a hypothetical, amid ongoing tension with Iran. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/security-aviation/2026-11-10/ty-article-magazine/.premium/drones-from-within-could-iran-launch-its-own-operation-spiderweb-in-israel/000001a1-bffe-d7bb-a7a5-ffff7e900000))

**AI-aided hack breaches German web host through its own AI-assistant tool.** German web hosting company Mittwald disclosed that hackers, allegedly assisted by AI, broke in through a feature meant to let customers connect AI assistants to their hosting accounts. *Why it matters:* it's an early real-world case of attackers using a company's own AI-integration tooling as the entry point, a weakness security researchers have been warning about. ([The Record](https://therecord.media/cyberattack-germany-ai-mittwald))
