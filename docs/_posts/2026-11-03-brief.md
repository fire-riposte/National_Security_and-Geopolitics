**Bottom line:** The US struck Iran's Qeshm Island overnight, a limited defensive strike that came hours after Trump said he doubted he'd resume a full campaign against Iran after Tuesday's midterms, leaving Washington and Israel still split on timing of any larger strike. Washington warned Germany's BND it may review intelligence-sharing after the arrest of former BND chief August Hanning on espionage charges, and the Royal Navy disclosed three separate operations in 17 days tracking Russian naval and intelligence-gathering activity near the UK.

## Espionage and counterintelligence

**The Royal Navy tracked Russian intelligence-gathering and naval activity in UK waters across three separate operations over 17 days.** Helicopters and warships monitored the Russian intelligence-gathering ship Viktor Leonov as it shadowed NATO's Exercise Strike Warrior, a drill involving about 2,000 personnel from six nations. Separately, HMS Dauntless and HMS Tyne tracked the frigate Neustrashimy as it escorted sanctioned cargo ships through the English Channel, and HMS Tyne then shadowed the landing ship Aleksandr Shabalin as it escorted another cargo vessel and a tug. The Royal Navy said its activations to monitor Russian activity are up 25% this year compared with 2025. *Why it matters:* The pace shows Russian naval and intelligence activity near UK waters, including sanctions-busting cargo escorts, has kept rising despite repeated public tracking and warnings. ([Royal Navy](https://www.royalnavy.mod.uk/news/2026/november/02/20261102-royal-navy-tracks-russian-vessels-across-october), [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/uk-intercepts-russian-spy-ship-monitoring-nato-military-exercises/))

**US intelligence agencies warned Germany's BND they may review cooperation after the arrest of former BND chief August Hanning on espionage charges.** Hanning, 80, who led Germany's foreign intelligence service from 1998 to 2005, was arrested Oct. 6 on suspicion of acting as a foreign intelligence agent, treasonous espionage and illegally obtaining state secrets; prosecutors allege a former aide passed him hundreds of classified BND documents in exchange for payment over roughly a decade. Citing government sources, reports say Washington signaled a possible review of the relationship but would keep sharing counterterrorism data. *Why it matters:* The case has already unsettled European intelligence services worried about damage to Germany's intelligence-sharing relationships. ([UNN](https://unn.ua/en/news/the-us-may-restrict-intelligence-sharing-with-germany-over-a-scandal-involving-a-former-intelligence-chief), [KUNC](https://www.kunc.org/2026-10-14/germanys-intelligence-scandal-has-a-long-list-of-possible-consequences))

**Russia's FSB said it arrested a minor over a threat to attack a Moscow park, crediting a tip from US authorities.** The Moscow Times reported the arrest came after what Russian authorities described as a US tip-off, though details of the alleged plot remain thin. *Why it matters:* The case, as reported by Russian authorities, points to a narrow channel of US-Russia security cooperation continuing even as the two countries clash over Ukraine and elsewhere. ([The Moscow Times](https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2026/11/01/fsb-arrests-minor-over-moscow-park-attack-threat-after-us-tip-off-a94148))

## US military and intelligence community

**The yearlong war with Iran has left a key US Gulf base barely functional, The Cipher Brief reported.** Naval Support Activity Bahrain and the Fifth Fleet headquarters have been largely inoperable for their original functions since Iranian strikes, with repairs estimated at $400 million. Chief of Naval Operations Admiral Daryl Caudle and other senior defense officials said the Navy does not plan to rebuild the base to its prior role, citing the risk of another strike. *Why it matters:* The lingering damage shows the conflict with Iran has degraded US forward posture in the Gulf, not just Iranian capabilities. ([The Cipher Brief](https://www.thecipherbrief.com/hidden-cost-war-with-iran))

## China, Russia, Iran and North Korea

**The US struck Iran's Qeshm Island overnight, hours after Trump said he doubted a new strike campaign, as Washington and Israel stay split on timing of any larger action.** The strike was part of a series of limited attacks in recent weeks, Haaretz reported, citing the Wall Street Journal. Hours earlier, Trump had said he was not considering a resumption of strikes after Tuesday's midterm elections and floated a possible deal with Tehran, brokered with Qatari help. Separately, Israeli officials say the IDF is reluctant to join a larger joint US-Israeli strike on Iran while Prime Minister Netanyahu's coalition talks continue, though former chief of staff Gadi Eisenkot has signaled he would back the IDF chief if Netanyahu pushes to escalate. *Why it matters:* The mixed signals show Washington and Jerusalem still haven't settled on whether, or when, to resume a wider campaign against Iran once the midterms are over. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/middle-east-news/iran/2026-11-03/ty-article/report-u-s-strikes-irans-qeshm-island-u-s-officials-say/000001a1-9ef4-de48-abb1-dff57b640000), [Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/us-news/2026-11-03/ty-article/trump-raises-doubts-about-renewed-attacks-on-iran/000001a1-9e92-d02e-abe7-df9643660000), [Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/israel-security/2026-11-03/ty-article/.premium/idf-hesitant-to-launch-joint-u-s-israeli-strike-in-iran-during-coalition-talks/000001a1-9daa-d02e-abe7-ddaeddc20000))

**China's new drone carrier launched and recovered a stealth combat drone for the first time.** The Chinese navy's Type 076 drone carrier Sichuan carried out flight operations with the GJ-21 stealth reconnaissance-strike drone, USNI News reported, in the vessel's latest step toward becoming operational. *Why it matters:* The milestone shows China moving a new class of warship built around uncrewed combat aircraft toward real capability. ([USNI News](https://news.usni.org/2026/11/02/chinas-new-drone-carrier-launches-recovers-first-autonomous-combat-aircraft))

**Lebanon's central bank revoked the license of a major Iranian-owned bank, testing Hezbollah's parallel finance network.** The Alma Center reported that Lebanon's central bank pulled the operating license of Bank Saderat Iran, which had operated in the country since 1962, with the bigger test being whether Lebanon can also act against Hezbollah's al-Qard al-Hassan financial network. *Why it matters:* The move is a rare case of Lebanese authorities acting directly against Iranian financial infrastructure tied to Hezbollah. ([Alma Center](https://israel-alma.org/lebanon-revokes-bank-saderat-irans-license-but-the-real-test-is-hezbollahs-al-qard-al-hassan/))

**Russia and Ukraine are each adapting their drone warfare to counter the other's defenses.** Russia is fitting its Shahed drones with bigger antennas to beat Ukrainian jamming, Defense News reported, with Kyiv now needing roughly twice as many jammers to stop each upgraded drone. Separately, a small Ukrainian-made receiver can intercept Russian drone video feeds in real time, letting Ukrainian operators see what Russian drone crews see. *Why it matters:* The back-and-forth shows electronic warfare, not just missiles and drones themselves, has become a central front in the war. ([Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/global/europe/2026/11/02/russia-is-giving-its-drones-bigger-antennas-to-beat-ukrainian-jamming/), [Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/industry/techwatch/2026/11/02/tiny-ukrainian-receiver-intercepts-russian-drone-feeds-in-real-time/))

## Germany and Israel

**Israel's Shin Bet chief reportedly considered delaying official election results over unproven foreign-interference claims, Haaretz reported.** Shin Bet chief David Zini dropped the idea only several days after the vote, senior law enforcement sources told Haaretz, after being told drastic steps could not be taken without solid evidence. The report comes as the Shin Bet and Mossad near completion of a widened probe into alleged foreign election interference. *Why it matters:* Critics say Netanyahu has used the Shin Bet and the unproven interference claims to cast doubt on a vote he could lose. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/elections/2026-11-02/ty-article/.premium/sources-shin-bet-head-sought-results-delay-over-alleged-foreign-influence/000001a1-9c26-d216-a5af-ffb78dd20000))

**The IDF arrested 18 Israeli settlers during a forced evacuation of an illegal West Bank outpost.** Police said the settlers assaulted and obstructed security forces near the city of Salfit after arriving at the site to try to disrupt the evacuation. *Why it matters:* The clash shows friction between Israeli security forces and settlers persisting even as the military presses its broader West Bank campaign. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/israel-security/2026-11-02/ty-article/.premium/idf-says-it-arrested-18-settlers-during-illegal-west-bank-outpost-evacuation/000001a1-9d26-db9a-a1b3-9d36a2030000))
