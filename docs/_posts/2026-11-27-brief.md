**Bottom line:** US intelligence and administration officials are publicly split over whether a Chinese businesswoman linked to Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang was acting as a Beijing intelligence asset, the latest sign of how far the chip-export fight with China has reached into Washington. Hackers separately breached an unclassified CIA and intelligence-community system, and Microsoft says Chinese and North Korean hackers are now using a new AI tool that can break into networks within minutes. In Israel, Mossad chief Roman Gofman is pushing for a unilateral strike on Iran if the US won't join one, a position IDF Chief of Staff Eyal Zamir has resisted.

## Espionage and counterintelligence

**US agencies publicly split over Nvidia-linked Chinese spy claim.** US intelligence and administration officials disagree over whether Alice Huang, a Chinese businesswoman with access to Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, was acting as a Chinese intelligence asset. The New York Times reported that Secretary of State Marco Rubio briefed Trump on the case in more assertive terms than the underlying intelligence supported, while CIA officials have said they aren't convinced the evidence shows she knowingly passed information to Beijing. The FBI and the Justice Department say they have reached no conclusion. *Why it matters:* a public rift among the top US intelligence and law enforcement agencies over a case touching the world's most valuable chipmaker complicates any unified policy response to Beijing.
([TechSpot](https://techspot.com/news/114483-us-officials-chinese-businesswoman-close-nvidia-jensen-huang.html), [IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.sg/A000P2J))

**Hackers breach unclassified CIA, intelligence-community research system.** Hackers breached an unclassified system used by the CIA and the Office of the Director of National Intelligence for open-source research. A source told CyberScoop no other systems are currently at risk. *Why it matters:* even a breach confined to an unclassified system raises questions about security practices on networks that touch the intelligence community.
([CyberScoop](https://cyberscoop.com/cia-odni-osint-data-breach/))

**Chinese, North Korean hackers adopt new AI tool for fast network breaches.** Microsoft says an AI tool called ARTEX is being rapidly integrated into the hacking toolkits of state-linked groups from China, including the Salt Typhoon campaign, and North Korea, letting attackers gain network access within minutes. *Why it matters:* AI tools are lowering the skill and time needed for state-backed cyber espionage against poorly defended targets.
([The Record](https://therecord.media/microsoft-china-salt-typhoon-using-artex-ai-tool))

**Active-duty Army sergeant pleads guilty to passing defense information to China.** Jian Zhao, 37, an active-duty Army sergeant stationed at Joint Base Lewis-McChord, pleaded guilty to conspiring to gather and transfer US national defense information to individuals connected to China, the Justice Department said. *Why it matters:* another insider case shows Beijing continuing to target active-duty US service members for recruitment.
([Justice Department](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/active-duty-us-army-sergeant-pleads-guilty-conspiracy-gather-and-transfer-national-defense))

**MI5 warns UK defense firms of Russian sabotage risk.** The UK's domestic intelligence service MI5 warned defense contractors of a heightened risk of Russian sabotage, urging firms to reduce how easily hostile actors can identify them as targets, conduct reconnaissance or attack their people and sites, Breaking Defense reported. *Why it matters:* a formal warning from a major Western intelligence service signals the sabotage threat to defense industry is spreading beyond NATO's eastern frontline states.
([Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/11/uks-mi5-warns-local-defense-firms-of-russian-sabotage-risk/))

**Violent attacks on Beijing's critics in Taiwan raise transnational repression concerns.** A wave of attacks on Taiwan-based critics of China, including an assault on comedian Huang Yi-hao, has raised alarm over Chinese transnational repression, 1News reported. Taiwan's government says China has carried out at least 149 such incidents against 131 people since 2005; Beijing denies involvement and calls the claims a fabrication by Taiwan's ruling party. *Why it matters:* the violence suggests Beijing's intimidation campaign against its critics is extending from surveillance and harassment into physical attacks on Taiwanese soil.
([1News](https://www.1news.co.nz/2026/11/15/violent-attacks-on-china-critics-in-taiwan-raise-repression-concerns/))

## US military and intelligence community

**Navy pilot retention crisis surfaces at Hung Cao's confirmation hearing.** Testimony at Hung Cao's Senate confirmation hearing laid out a Navy pilot retention crisis, with the Chief of Naval Personnel warning of a possible drawdown of 15 percent or more in advanced fighter pilots as aviators leave for industry jobs, The Cipher Brief reported. *Why it matters:* losing experienced fighter pilots at that rate would erode the Navy's ability to project airpower even as it faces other readiness pressures.
([The Cipher Brief](https://www.thecipherbrief.com/the-navys-problems-come-into-focus-at-hung-caos-confirmation-hearing))

**GAO report flags persistent Pentagon munitions shortages.** A Government Accountability Office report submitted to the House Armed Services Committee found persistent shortages in US munitions stockpiles and said the Department of Defense needs better planning and leadership to address them, according to USNI News. *Why it matters:* unresolved munitions shortfalls could limit the US ability to sustain a prolonged conflict or arm allies simultaneously.
([USNI News](https://news.usni.org/2026/11/26/gao-on-how-the-defense-department-can-address-munitions-shortages))

## China, Russia, Iran and North Korea

**Huawei and subsidiaries convicted in US sanctions and trade-secrets trial.** A federal jury in the Eastern District of New York convicted Huawei Technologies and three subsidiaries of violating US sanctions on Iran, lying to banks, laundering money and stealing trade secrets, following a three-month trial, the Justice Department said. *Why it matters:* the conviction is one of the most significant US legal wins yet against a Chinese tech giant central to the fight over advanced semiconductors.
([Justice Department](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/chinese-tech-giant-huawei-and-subsidiaries-convicted-sanctions-violations-fraud-conspiracy))

**Estimated Russian deaths in Ukraine surpass combined US toll since World War One.** More than 700,000 Russian soldiers have been killed in Ukraine since Moscow's full-scale invasion nearly five years ago, surpassing the combined US death toll from every war since World War One, according to fresh estimates reported by Defense News. *Why it matters:* the toll underscores how much manpower Russia has been willing to absorb to keep the war going.
([Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/global/europe/2026/11/25/estimates-of-russians-killed-in-ukraine-surpass-all-combined-us-war-deaths-since-wwi/))

**NATO sees no Russian naval buildup in Mediterranean after strike near Libya.** NATO says it sees no Russian naval buildup in the Mediterranean following Ukraine's strike on a Russian-linked ship near Libya, though analysts warn Moscow could still retaliate and tensions along NATO's southern flank could rise, Breaking Defense reported. *Why it matters:* Ukraine's expanding naval campaign keeps raising the risk of the war spreading geographically, even without an immediate Russian response.
([Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/11/nato-sees-no-russian-naval-buildup-in-the-med-after-strike-near-libya-but-analysts-warn-of-wider-risks/))

## Germany and Israel

**Mossad chief pushes for unilateral Iran strike as IDF chief favors waiting.** Mossad chief Roman Gofman has pushed Washington to back a unilateral Israeli strike on Iran if the US won't join one, while IDF Chief of Staff Eyal Zamir has favored waiting for a decision from Trump, according to Al-Monitor and other Israeli outlets citing sources familiar with internal discussions. *Why it matters:* a public divide between Israel's top intelligence chief and its top general could shape whether Israel acts alone against Iran in the coming weeks.
([Al-Monitor](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/11/israeli-spy-chief-lobbied-joint-iran-strikes-us-visit-sources-say))

**Bundeswehr soldiers help Poland fortify border with Russia's Kaliningrad exclave.** About 40 Bundeswehr soldiers have been helping Poland build fortifications, including anti-tank barriers and trenches, near the border with Russia's Kaliningrad exclave as part of Poland's "East Shield" project, deutschland.de reported. *Why it matters:* it's a concrete German troop contribution to hardening NATO's eastern flank, not just funding.
([deutschland.de](https://www.deutschland.de/en/news/bundeswehr-supports-poland-with-protecting-natos-eastern-border))

**Israel raises defense budget by $5 billion.** Israel increased its defense budget by $5 billion, the latest in a series of increases this year, even as officials voice concern about funding priorities beyond 2026, Breaking Defense reported. *Why it matters:* sustained defense increases after prolonged conflict could squeeze other government spending priorities.
([Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/11/israel-increases-defense-budget-by-5-billion-as-concerns-continue-over-future-funding/))

**Bundeswehr tests Ukraine's DELTA battlefield system.** The German Army is testing Ukraine's DELTA battlefield management system, which fuses drone, sensor and unit data into a single picture, and Defense Minister Boris Pistorius says Germany wants to help develop it further with Ukraine, Militarnyi reported. The Bundeswehr's Cyber Innovation Hub is studying how to integrate it. *Why it matters:* adopting a battle-tested Ukrainian system could give the Bundeswehr faster situational awareness than building an equivalent from scratch.
([Militarnyi](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/bundeswehr-interest-ukrainian-delta-system/))
