**Bottom line:** Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's confirmed affair with a suspected Chinese intelligence asset has turned a chip-export dispute into a full-blown scandal, with China calling the underlying spying claims a smear and Congress still waiting on a briefing. US Central Command stood up a new task force to choke off Iran's land supply routes after fresh US strikes hit an Iranian air defense system on Qeshm Island, even as Trump says a renewed strike campaign is "highly unlikely" if talks progress. In Israel, both Shin Bet and the attorney general now say they have found no evidence backing the foreign election-interference claims that have consumed the agency for a week.

## Espionage and counterintelligence

**Jensen Huang's affair with a suspected Chinese spy escalates into a national-security scare.** Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang has confirmed a personal affair with Alice Huang (no relation), a Singapore-based Megaspeed executive US intelligence has assessed as a Chinese intelligence asset with access to him, the BBC reported. China's foreign ministry has dismissed the underlying espionage claims as "groundless speculation" meant to "smear China," while three senior House Republicans are still seeking a closed-door congressional briefing from Huang over the matter. *Why it matters:* a personal scandal involving Nvidia's chief is now entangled with the broader fight over AI chip exports to China. ([BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ckgel338xql3o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss), [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/videos/cq3989nkzn3ko?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss), [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/aseanplus/aseanplus-news/2026/10/30/china-dismisses-us-report-of-suspected-spy-targeting-nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-as-groundless))

**Ex-FBI counterintelligence agent pleads guilty to stealing $2.4 million from nation-state hackers.** Patrick Steven Yaroch, 37, admitted using his position on an FBI investigative squad to steal more than $2.4 million in cryptocurrency that had been seized from nation-state hacking groups, to cover his own debts. He faces up to 20 years in prison on wire fraud and money laundering charges. *Why it matters:* a counterintelligence agent turning the bureau's own seized assets into personal theft is a reminder that insider risk runs both ways. ([The Record](https://therecord.media/fbi-agent-pleads-guilty-to-stealing-from-nation-state-hackers))

**Pro-Russian hackers leak Italian foreign minister's personal data.** Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni said pro-Russian hackers published personal data belonging to Foreign Minister Antonio Tajani on the dark web. Rome has not disclosed the scope of the breach or named the group responsible. *Why it matters:* it's the latest in a pattern of pro-Russian hacking groups targeting European officials involved in Ukraine policy. ([BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cr20wvge32j3o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss), [CTV News](https://www.ctvnews.ca/world/article/italy-pm-says-pro-russian-hackers-leaked-foreign-ministers-data-on-dark-web))

**China's network of overseas "police stations" grows to nearly 1,250.** A new tally finds China has expanded its network of unofficial overseas police service stations to roughly 1,250 locations worldwide, including at least 18 in Canada. The stations are nominally for consular services like renewing documents, but researchers and Western governments say they're also used to pressure and monitor Chinese diaspora communities. *Why it matters:* it's a large-scale transnational repression effort operating mostly outside normal diplomatic channels. ([CTV News](https://www.ctvnews.ca/canada/article/report-alleges-china-linked-to-at-least-18-overseas-police-stations-in-canada/))

## US military and intelligence community

**CENTCOM stands up Task Force Iron Gate to choke off Iran's land supply routes.** US Central Command (CENTCOM) said Saturday it launched Task Force Iron Gate, an intra-agency effort to "constrain Iran's ability to replenish its military" by disrupting arms shipments moving over land. Iran's Revolutionary Guards dismissed the task force as "wordplay" that won't succeed in forcing an economic collapse. *Why it matters:* it shows Washington shifting toward a longer-term pressure campaign on Iran's supply chain rather than relying only on strikes. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/middle-east-news/iran/2026-11-07/ty-article/u-s-military-launches-task-force-to-restrict-irans-land-based-arms-trade/000001a1-b663-d146-ada9-fe7f731f0000), [Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/middle-east-news/iran/2026-11-08/ty-article/iran-dismisses-new-u-s-task-force-aimed-at-blocking-its-trade-routes/000001a1-bab4-d903-a3b3-bbbfed440000))

**Space Force satellites can't talk to each other, and the fix may cost $1 billion.** Key Space Force satellites lack the ability to communicate directly with one another, sources told Breaking Defense, likely requiring a roughly $1 billion solution involving 12 to 13 additional "Space Exchange Point" satellites to translate between SpaceX's SDN Backbone and other Pentagon communications and missile-tracking constellations. *Why it matters:* it's a basic interoperability gap in a satellite architecture the Pentagon is counting on for missile defense and command and control. ([Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/11/key-space-force-satellites-cant-talk-to-each-other-likely-requiring-1b-solution-sources/))

## China, Russia, Iran and North Korea

**New US strikes hit Iranian air defenses on Qeshm Island.** Iranian state TV reported explosions on Qeshm Island and in southern cities after President Trump said the US military carried out new limited strikes on Iran on Friday, targeting an Iranian air defense system. *Why it matters:* it shows the US is still conducting kinetic strikes even as Trump publicly floats a negotiated end to the conflict. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/middle-east-news/iran/2026-11-07/ty-article/.premium/explosions-heard-on-irans-qeshm-island-and-southern-cities-state-tv-reports/000001a1-b7a5-d1cd-aded-bfa79b250000))

**FBI warns Iran considered a drone attack on California.** The FBI alerted California law enforcement that Iran had "aspired" to retaliate for US strikes with a drone attack launched from an unidentified vessel off the coast, targeting unspecified sites, according to an alert reviewed by ABC News. The bureau said it has no further details on timing, method or perpetrators, and officials believe the recent bombardment of Iran has degraded its capacity to carry out such an attack. *Why it matters:* even an unverified, degraded threat against the US homeland changes the calculus for domestic security agencies. ([ABC7](https://abc7news.com/post/fbi-warns-iran-aspired-attack-california-drones-retaliation-war-alert/18703314/))

**Russia fields a homegrown Starlink rival for military communications over Ukraine.** Ukrainian military intelligence says Russia is using its new Rassvet satellite network, billed as a domestic answer to Starlink, for military communications over the battlefield in Ukraine. *Why it matters:* it suggests Moscow has found a way to reduce its reliance on commercial and improvised communications systems that Ukraine has learned to target. ([Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/industry/techwatch/2026/11/06/russia-using-own-version-of-starlink-for-military-comms-over-ukraine-kyiv-says/))

**Russian strikes wound nine and damage Ukraine's power grid ahead of trilateral talks.** Russian strikes wounded at least nine people and damaged part of Ukraine's power network, the BBC reported, days before Ukrainian and Russian delegations are due to hold trilateral talks with the US in Abu Dhabi. *Why it matters:* Moscow is still hitting Ukrainian infrastructure heading into talks meant to de-escalate the war. ([BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cm4g1nq8158po?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss))

## Germany and Israel

**Shin Bet and Israel's attorney general say they've found no evidence of foreign election interference.** Sources linked to Shin Bet told Haaretz that investigators examining hundreds of thousands of shekels allegedly transferred from the Palestinian Authority have found no evidence the money bought votes, and Israel's attorney general separately said she is "not aware" of any evidence of interference. The admissions come a week into an investigation led by Shin Bet chief David Zini, who has removed internal critics and installed an ally to run the probe. *Why it matters:* a politically charged probe that produced no evidence after a week risks further damaging Shin Bet's credibility as an independent agency. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/elections/2026-11-09/ty-article/.premium/shin-bet-yet-to-foreign-election-interference-evidence-officials-tell-haaretz/000001a1-bf56-dc4a-a7a9-bff7b14a0000), [Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/israel-politics/2026-11-08/ty-article/.premium/israels-ag-not-aware-of-evidence-of-election-interference-amid-shin-bet-probe/000001a1-bbc8-dda4-adb7-bbcc31670000))

**Israel Defense Forces expelled 3,300 Palestinians from their Hebron-area homes, found four guns.** The Israeli military (IDF) expelled residents of three Hebron neighborhoods under cover of darkness for ten days, in response to an attack carried out by two local residents, before allowing them to return this week. The search turned up four firearms. *Why it matters:* the scale of the expulsion relative to what was found raises collective-punishment concerns under international law. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/twilight-zone/2026-11-07/ty-article-magazine/.highlight/the-idf-forced-3-300-palestinians-from-their-homes-only-to-find-four-guns/000001a1-b542-defa-a7f7-bd7f71ed0000))

**Pistorius says the Bundeswehr doesn't need conscription for now.** German Defense Minister Boris Pistorius said in a Deutschlandfunk interview that he sees no current need to reinstate compulsory military service, saying the Bundeswehr's voluntary recruitment model, including a questionnaire sent to young people, should be enough for now. *Why it matters:* it signals Pistorius wants to avoid the political fight over conscription while still growing the force. ([Augen geradeaus!, in German](https://augengeradeaus.net/2026/11/pistorius-sieht-vorerst-keine-notwendigkeit-fuer-wehrpflicht-fragebogen-nicht-fuer-nachwuchswerbung/))
