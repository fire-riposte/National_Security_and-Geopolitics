**Bottom line:** British police, working with MI5 and the FBI, arrested a US Army officer assigned to a NATO headquarters in England on suspicion of spying for Russia, and officials say he was stopped before passing anything along. Nvidia's Jensen Huang abruptly resigned as CEO amid reports his affairs included a woman accused of ties to Chinese intelligence, and lawmakers have summoned him to testify. Separately, the FBI says North Korean hackers used deepfake video calls to steal more than $400 million in crypto, and a cyberattack disguised as a software update briefly grounded Germany's reconnaissance drone fleet.

## Espionage and counterintelligence

**US Army officer arrested in England on suspicion of spying for Russia.** British police, working with the domestic security service MI5 and the FBI, arrested a 41-year-old US Army officer on November 10 near Gloucester on suspicion of violating the UK's National Security Act 2023 by assisting a foreign intelligence service and handling protected information. He was one of the US personnel assigned to Imjin Barracks, which hosts a NATO rapid-reaction corps headquarters. Officials said no sensitive information reached Russia, suggesting he was under surveillance before any handoff, and FBI Director Kash Patel called it an "important arrest." Police said the case is unrelated to the separate Iran-linked plot against RAF Fairford. *Why it matters:* an alleged insider inside a NATO command structure is a reminder that rapid-reaction headquarters are a recruitment target, even if this one appears to have been caught early. ([AP via Anchorage Daily News](https://www.adn.com/alaska-news/military/2026/11/10/us-army-officer-arrested-in-uk-on-suspicion-of-trying-to-steal-secrets-for-russia/), [VOA](https://www.voanews.com/a/usa_former-us-army-officer-arrested-charges-spying-russia/6194805.html))

**Nvidia's Jensen Huang resigns as CEO amid Chinese espionage allegations.** Huang abruptly quit on Thursday after reports of extramarital affairs, including with a woman identified in Taiwanese and Chinese press as "Alice Huang," who multiple outlets allege has ties to Chinese intelligence or chip-smuggling networks; those ties remain unconfirmed. US lawmakers have summoned Huang to testify before Congress about the relationship and about Nvidia's safeguards against foreign espionage and chip diversion to China. *Why it matters:* a chipmaker CEO's personal entanglement becoming a national-security question shows how exposed the AI and semiconductor industry is to influence operations, on top of the existing export-control fight with Beijing. ([Foreign Policy](https://foreignpolicy.com/2026/11/12/nvidia-jensen-huang-resigns-ai-china-spy-megaspeed-extramarital-affairs/), [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmq5vd1wdgz6o))

**FBI says North Korean hackers stole $400 million with deepfake video calls; feds claw back $1.2 billion from an earlier heist.** The FBI says hackers linked to APT38, a North Korean state hacking group, posed as trusted contacts on video calls using face-swapping tools to steal more than $400 million in cryptocurrency over the past 18 months. Separately, the Justice Department and FBI announced the seizure of $1.2 billion in cryptocurrency tied to an earlier North Korea-linked theft from Binance, and said they dismantled the "FaceStealer" tools behind it. *Why it matters:* crypto theft is a major funding stream for North Korea's weapons programs, and this is one of the larger law-enforcement clawbacks against it. ([BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqdj1ellpd70o), [CyberScoop](https://cyberscoop.com/us-seizes-north-korea-hacking-infrastructure-binance/), [The Record](https://therecord.media/stolen-binance-crypto-recovered-doj-facestealer-disrupted))

**Two Iranian men convicted in UK of spying on dissident journalists.** A British court convicted two Iranian men of spying on London-based dissident journalists and a lawyer on behalf of Tehran, with prosecutors alleging the pair also planned a violent attack. The convictions are the first under the National Security Act 2023 provisions targeting foreign-state threat activity. *Why it matters:* it shows Iran's transnational repression campaign against exiled critics reaching into Britain, and a UK legal system now set up to prosecute it as a national-security offense rather than ordinary crime. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/world-news/europe/2026-11-13/ty-article/two-iranian-men-convicted-of-spying-on-dissident-journalists-in-the-u-k/000001a1-d3e9-da30-a7fb-dbfdd0bb0000))

**Three Iran-backed men indicted over alleged plot against RAF Fairford, one directing it from a Brooklyn jail cell.** Prosecutors say Mohammad Baqer Saad Dawood al-Saadi, already charged over plots against Jewish targets in Europe and the US, used family phone calls from jail in Brooklyn to direct two Kataib Hezbollah associates toward an attack on the UK's RAF Fairford air base, part of an alleged plan for about 20 attacks across the US and Europe. *Why it matters:* it shows an Iran-aligned militia network can keep directing operations against a Western military target even with its alleged leader already in US custody. ([Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/global/europe/2026/11/12/3-iranian-backed-men-indicted-in-attempted-plot-on-raf-fairford/), [Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/us-news/2026-11-13/ty-article/.premium/militia-commander-plotted-attack-on-u-k-air-base-from-brooklyn-jail-u-s-says/000001a1-d1c2-d292-abf9-fdff5cee0000))

**Syrian Electronic Army hacker sentenced to 42 months after evading US authorities for a decade.** Ahmad Umar Agha, known online as "Th3Pr0," was sentenced in a Virginia federal court for a hacking conspiracy the Justice Department says ran from 2011 to 2024 and was directed by members of Bashar al-Assad's inner circle, including the chief of Syria's General Intelligence Directorate. His best-known hack was a fake 2013 Associated Press tweet claiming the White House had been bombed, which briefly wiped $136 billion off the stock market. *Why it matters:* it is a rare conviction of a state-linked hacking crew more than a decade after the fact, and a reminder of how long such networks can evade capture. ([Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/30/syrian-hacker-sentenced-42-months-prison/), [The Record](https://therecord.media/syrian-electronic-army-hacker-sentenced-three-years))

## US military and intelligence community

**Pentagon "greenlights" a permanent US troop presence in Poland.** Poland's defense minister said the Pentagon has approved a permanent US troop presence in the country, with the White House to decide on a specific site by early December. *Why it matters:* it would mark a shift from a rotational US presence to a permanent one on NATO's eastern flank. ([Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/global/europe/2026/11/12/pentagon-greenlit-permanent-presence-of-us-troops-in-poland-official-says/))

**Civil liberties groups urge Congress not to bury Section 702 surveillance renewal inside the defense bill.** A coalition is pressing Hill leaders not to fold reauthorization of Section 702 of the Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act into the National Defense Authorization Act, arguing the warrantless surveillance authority deserves standalone debate. *Why it matters:* Section 702 lets intelligence agencies collect foreign targets' communications but also sweeps in Americans' data, and attaching it to a must-pass defense bill would limit scrutiny. ([CyberScoop](https://cyberscoop.com/section-702-reauthorization-ndaa-debate/))

**Navy's next attack submarine is designed as a mothership for undersea drones.** A Navy requirements official said the SSN(X) attack submarine program is being designed to be faster, quicker to repair, and able to serve as a mothership for a constellation of undersea drones, with the analysis of alternatives nearing completion. *Why it matters:* it signals the Navy is building unmanned systems into the core of its next-generation submarine design, aimed partly at countering China's undersea buildup. ([USNI News](https://news.usni.org/2026/11/12/ssnx-attack-boat-will-be-a-swift-lethal-mothership-for-undersea-drones-navy-says))

## China, Russia, Iran and North Korea

**Taiwan says China is running "area denial" drills in the Western Pacific.** Taiwan's defense minister, Wellington Koo, told lawmakers China's Liaoning carrier group and other naval formations are conducting "area denial" exercises meant to restrict outside forces' access to the waters around Taiwan; Taiwan has flagged more than 100 People's Liberation Army naval and coast guard vessels involved, though Beijing has not announced the drills. *Why it matters:* analysts see the drills as rehearsal for isolating Taiwan from outside military support in a future conflict. ([Taipei Times](https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/front/archives/2026/11/11/2003867520), [Modern Diplomacy](https://moderndiplomacy.eu/2026/11/10/is-china-preparing-to-block-us-military-support-for-taiwan-with-western-pacific-naval-drills/))

**Russian warship struck by Ukrainian drone in the open Baltic Sea.** A Russian warship was hit by a Ukrainian drone in the Gulf of Finland, which the BBC says is the first time Ukraine has attacked a Russian ship in open Baltic waters rather than closer to Ukrainian or Black Sea territory; Finland's military separately confirmed detecting "a strike" on a Russian warship in the Baltic, while saying the regional security situation remains stable. *Why it matters:* it extends Ukraine's long-range strike campaign into waters bordering NATO members, raising the odds of an incident near alliance territory. ([BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmn0d33zz34zo), [Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/global/europe/2026/11/12/finland-says-it-detects-strike-on-russian-warship-in-baltic-sea/))

**Putin agrees to let Europe into the next round of Ukraine peace talks as Kyiv reports renewed Starshield disruptions.** A US envoy said President Vladimir Putin agreed to let European representatives join the next round of Ukraine peace talks. Ukraine separately said disruptions to the Starshield satellite service are back, the second such report in a month; SpaceX denies the system has been altered. *Why it matters:* it is a diplomatic opening on talks while raising fresh doubts about the reliability of US satellite support to Ukraine during the negotiations. ([Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/global/2026/11/12/putin-lets-europe-join-ukraine-peace-talks-as-kyiv-says-starshield-disrupted-again/), [The War Zone](https://www.twz.com/news-features/ukraine-says-starshield-disruptions-are-back-as-u-s-negotiators-meet-with-putin))

## Germany and Israel

**Cyberattack disguised as a software update briefly grounds Bundeswehr's reconnaissance drone fleet.** Attackers planted malware disguised as a routine update to "QBase," the mission-planning software for Quantum Systems' Vector drone, which the German military operates as the "Falke" reconnaissance system. Quantum Systems says it found the intrusion in late October and that the manipulated update reached some customer servers but was caught before reaching the drones themselves; the Bundeswehr grounded its fleet as a precaution, and the company says the issue is now contained and its drones are safe to fly, though Germany's defense ministry has not confirmed that. No attacker has been identified. *Why it matters:* it exposes a software supply-chain weakness in a drone system the German military depends on for reconnaissance, made by a company that also supplies drones to Ukraine. ([DroneXL](https://dronexl.co/2026/11/12/quantum-systems-qbase-malware-bundeswehr-grounds-drones/), [Ruhr Nachrichten, in German](https://www.ruhrnachrichten.de/ueberregionales/quantum-systems-hacker-angriff-cyberattacke-drohnen-muenchen-bundeswehr-drohnenhersteller-w1273693-2002298697/))

**Israeli election chief says Shin Bet's foreign-interference probe still lacks "actual evidence."** Central Elections Committee chairman Noam Sohlberg said the Shin Bet's investigation into alleged foreign interference in Israeli politics warrants further work but has not yet produced "actual evidence," found no sign the attorney general rejected any Shin Bet request, and criticized leaks about the probe as harmful to it. *Why it matters:* a politically sensitive domestic-security investigation drawing public pushback over its evidentiary basis raises oversight questions ahead of Israeli elections. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/israel-politics/2026-11-12/ty-article/.premium/election-committee-chief-interference-probe-has-yet-to-yield-actual-evidence/000001a1-d08f-dff3-a5bb-dc9f1e110000))

**Former Mossad chief David Barnea set to brief German lawmakers amid calls for his arrest.** Barnea, who led Mossad during the October 7 attacks, is scheduled to brief German lawmakers at a Bundestag event hosted by the Green Party. The European Center for Constitutional and Human Rights says Germany is obligated under international law to arrest him over allegations tied to the conduct of the Gaza war. *Why it matters:* it tests the tension between Germany's intelligence-sharing relationship with Israel and legal demands being raised against a former Israeli spy chief on German soil. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/world-news/europe/2026-11-12/ty-article/.premium/ex-mossad-chief-to-speak-at-german-parliament-event-amid-calls-for-prosecution/000001a1-d06e-d292-abf9-fd7fe07b0000))
