**Bottom line:** Poland detained six people, four of them on espionage charges, for allegedly funneling military-grade components to Russia through Kaliningrad, while a Finnish court handed a Russian tanker captain more than three years in prison for deliberately cutting Baltic Sea cables. The FBI's attribution of the record $6.3 billion Binance theft to North Korean hackers drew a furious denial from Pyongyang, and three senior House Republicans want Nvidia's Jensen Huang to brief Congress on November 18 over an alleged Chinese intelligence operation targeting him. In Israel, Shin Bet chief David Zini's handling of an election-interference probe has turned much of the political spectrum against him.

## Espionage and counterintelligence

**Poland detains six over alleged Russian military-supply network.** Poland's interior minister, Tomasz Siemoniak, said authorities detained six people on November 5: five Polish nationals aged 25 to 52 and a 37-year-old Russian woman. Prosecutors allege the group illegally shipped military-grade materials, including cartridge cases and bullets, from Poland through Lithuania into Russia's Kaliningrad exclave; four of the six have been charged with working for Russian intelligence and placed in pretrial detention, while all six face organized-crime charges. Siemoniak said nearly 100 people have been named suspects in similar cases since January 2024. *Why it matters:* it's the latest sign of how deeply Russian intelligence has tried to penetrate supply chains along NATO's eastern flank. ([Meduza](https://meduza.io/en/news/2026/11/05/polish-authorities-detain-a-russian-woman-and-five-poles-on-suspicion-of-illegally-supplying-weapons-components-to-russia), [The Insider](https://theins.press/en/news/299240))

**Finland jails Russian tanker captain over Baltic cable sabotage.** A Helsinki court convicted the Russian captain and an Azerbaijani boatswain of the tanker Fitburg of aggravated sabotage for deliberately dragging their anchor roughly across the Gulf of Finland seabed in December 2025, damaging undersea cables and a gas pipeline during a voyage from St. Petersburg to Israel. The captain, Andrei Maksimenko, received three and a half years and the boatswain nearly three years, both unconditional sentences with immediate detention; the court rejected a defense argument that Finland lacked jurisdiction. *Why it matters:* it's a rare conviction in a wave of suspected shadow-fleet sabotage against Baltic infrastructure, after an earlier case against the tanker Eagle S was thrown out on jurisdictional grounds. ([Meduza](https://meduza.io/en/news/2026/11/05/finnish-court-sentences-russian-captain-to-3-5-years-for-deliberately-damaging-gulf-of-finland-cables), [Helsinki Times](https://helsinkitimes.fi/finland/finland-news/domestic/29457-fitburg-captain-and-boatswain-sentenced-to-prison-over-baltic-sea-cable-damage.html))

**FBI blames North Korea for record Binance heist; Pyongyang denies it.** The FBI said North Korea's TraderTraitor hacking group, also tied to last year's $1.5 billion Bybit theft, stole roughly $6.3 billion in crypto assets from Binance around October 29, in what would be the largest crypto theft on record. Blockchain-intelligence firm TRM Labs said it concurred with high confidence, and Binance said it has replaced about 90% of the stolen funds. Kim Yo Jong, the sister of North Korean leader Kim Jong Un, dismissed the FBI's attribution as "a trite provocation" and "absurd rhetoric" without offering a rebuttal. *Why it matters:* state-backed crypto theft remains a major funding stream for North Korea's weapons programs, and the denial follows Pyongyang's usual pattern after US attribution. ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/1/fbi-blames-north-korea-for-record-6-3bn-crypto-heist), [AJC/AP](https://www.ajc.com/news/2026/11/north-korea-denies-involvement-in-binance-hack-and-criticizes-fbi-probe-as-a-trite-provocation/))

**House Republicans summon Nvidia's Huang over China spy allegations.** Three senior House Republicans — the chairs of the Foreign Affairs, China Select, and Intelligence committees — sent Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang a letter dated November 4 requesting a closed-door briefing on November 18. The request follows a New York Times report that US intelligence assessed a Singapore-based executive close to Huang, Alice Huang, as a Chinese intelligence asset who had access to him; officials reportedly never gave Huang a defensive briefing on the matter. Nvidia didn't address the letter directly, saying Huang talks with "thousands of customer executives every year," and President Trump said the administration would look into the report "very closely." *Why it matters:* it puts a chip-export flashpoint with China directly in front of Congress's intelligence overseers. ([Al Arabiya/Reuters](https://english.alarabiya.net/business/technology/2026/11/05/key-republicans-seek-briefing-from-nvidia-ceo-over-chinese-spy-allegations-document), [AsiaOne/Reuters](https://www.asiaone.com/world/key-republicans-seek-briefing-nvidia-ceo-over-chinese-spy-allegations-document))

## US military and intelligence community

**Iran war is pulling US destroyers away from the Pacific.** Five of the ten destroyers homeported in Yokosuka, Japan, plus the Yokosuka-based carrier George Washington, are deployed to US Central Command's area covering the Middle East, and Navy officials have told some crews they won't return home until 2027. Pacific Forum analyst Ralph Cossa said roughly a third of the operational fleet is now tied down in the Middle East, which he called "a problem for our allies in the Far East" and a weakening of deterrence against China even as Beijing has reportedly been told to be ready to move on Taiwan by 2027. US Pacific Command says its Indo-Pacific commitment "remains steadfast." *Why it matters:* a prolonged Iran commitment is thinning the naval presence the Pentagon's own strategy says should be prioritized against China. ([Defense One](https://www.defenseone.com/threats/2026/11/war-iran-stripping-western-pacific-us-naval-forces/417079/))

**US agencies warn of hackers targeting refrigeration systems.** The FBI and other US agencies issued a warning that hackers are targeting internet-connected commercial refrigeration and freezer systems worldwide. The warning follows Nextgov/FCW reporting that officials suspect Iran-linked hackers caused freezer failures at US military commissaries. *Why it matters:* it's another case of a state-linked actor probing overlooked industrial and logistics equipment that feeds directly into military readiness. ([Defense One](https://www.defenseone.com/threats/2026/11/warn-refrigerator-hacks-attacks-military/417076/))

## China, Russia, Iran and North Korea

**Iran holds firm on Hormuz closure, sends nuclear proposal via Qatar.** A senior Iranian official said Thursday that Iran has sent a nuclear proposal to the United States through Qatari mediation, while also demanding the lifting of US sanctions, release of frozen assets and war reparations. The same official said the Strait of Hormuz would remain closed until Washington meets Tehran's conditions. Separately, satellite imagery reviewed by the BBC points to continued construction activity near Iranian missile bases, the latest in a months-long pattern of repair and reinforcement at sites damaged in earlier US and Israeli strikes. *Why it matters:* Tehran is using both negotiating leverage and demonstrated rebuilding capacity to shape any post-conflict settlement. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/middle-east-news/iran/2026-11-06/ty-article/iran-sent-nuclear-proposal-to-u-s-via-qatar-senior-iranian-official-says/000001a1-ae09-dd7a-a3ed-ef4969d10000), [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ckjrwx1r2d5ro))

**Kim Jong Un inspects new North Korean warship.** North Korean state media reported that Kim Jong Un inspected construction of a new warship at the Nampho shipyard, believed to be a roughly 10,000-ton cruiser, larger than the North's existing 5,000-ton destroyers. *Why it matters:* it's the latest step in North Korea's push to build larger, more capable surface combatants. ([USNI News](https://news.usni.org/2026/11/05/north-korean-leader-inspects-new-warship-under-construction-at-nampho-shipyard))

**Melania Trump hosts Russian and Ukrainian officials, calls for lasting truce.** The US first lady hosted Russian and Ukrainian officials and called for a lasting truce in Ukraine; she was credited broadly, though without specifics, for having played a role in a recent ceasefire. *Why it matters:* it signals a more visible diplomatic role for the first lady in efforts to stabilize the Ukraine ceasefire. ([BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c9p8g085mz07o))

## Germany and Israel

**Freuding named Germany's next military chief.** Lieutenant General Christian Freuding, currently chief of the German Army and previously head of the Bundeswehr's Ukraine special staff, will succeed Carsten Breuer as Inspector General of the Bundeswehr in February 2027. The appointment drew welcome from lawmakers across party lines. *Why it matters:* Freuding, closely associated with German military support for Ukraine, will now set the Bundeswehr's overall direction during its ongoing rearmament. ([Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/global/europe/2026/11/05/germany-names-army-chief-freuding-as-next-chief-of-defense/), [Augen geradeaus!, in German](https://augengeradeaus.net/2026/11/fuers-archiv-freuding-wird-neuer-generalinspekteur/))

**Shin Bet chief's election probe turns coalition and opposition against him.** Shin Bet chief David Zini's push to question Arab political leaders over suspected foreign election interference, and his sharp dispute with the attorney general over the agency's investigative powers, has drawn criticism from across Israel's political spectrum. Haaretz reported that two Bedouin activists say they were strip-searched and threatened during Shin Bet interrogations over suspected Qatari funding, with an official saying the agency presented no supporting evidence to the attorney general; separately, former Shin Bet chief Ami Ayalon and other former officials who reviewed the classified intelligence urged further inquiry but warned against casting doubt on the election results. *Why it matters:* the dispute over how Shin Bet handled the probe, more than the underlying interference claims, is now the story in Jerusalem. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/elections/2026-11-06/ty-article/.premium/shin-bet-chief-zini-has-united-israels-coalition-and-opposition-against-him/000001a1-ae35-d041-a5f9-ef35cb6f0000), [Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/elections/2026-11-06/ty-article-magazine/.premium/shin-bet-interrogates-threatens-bedouin-political-activists-over-election/000001a1-ac6c-d3b7-a5ab-feeeb1340000))

**Bundeswehr refers record 724 sexual misconduct cases to prosecutors.** Germany's defense ministry told Redaktionsnetzwerk Deutschland that the Bundeswehr passed 724 cases involving alleged violations of sexual self-determination to public prosecutors between 2023 and 2025. A separate hidden-field study into underreported incidents is due to be presented by Defense Minister Boris Pistorius in the coming days, alongside a new 24-hour hotline for victims. *Why it matters:* the figures suggest the problem is both larger and more persistent than the Bundeswehr's own annual reports have shown. ([Ludwigsburg24, in German](https://www.ludwigsburg24.com/bundeswehr-meldet-724-verdachtsfaelle-sexuellen-fehlverhaltens/), [Ad-hoc-news.de, in German](https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/politik/bundeswehr-gibt-724-faelle-sexuellen-fehlverhaltens-weiter/70466731))

**Bundeswehr's new attack helicopters still can't fire their weapons.** Germany's federal audit office found that the Bundeswehr's roughly €2.5 billion purchase of H145M light combat helicopters, meant to replace the aging Tiger, still lacks combat-ready armament. The report blames the Bundeswehr for underestimating how hard it would be to convert a civilian helicopter design into a true combat aircraft, and says ammunition for the onboard cannon still hasn't been tested, despite that testing having been planned since 2020. *Why it matters:* it's another case of a major German procurement program being delivered without the capability it was bought for. ([Blackout News, in German](https://blackout-news.de/aktuelles/bundeswehr-kauft-kampfhubschrauber-fuer-25-milliarden-euro-ohne-einsatzbereite-bewaffnung/))
