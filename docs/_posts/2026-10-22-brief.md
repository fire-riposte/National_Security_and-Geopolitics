## Espionage and counterintelligence

**Pentagon reportedly planning deep-cover spy program against China; Pentagon denies it.** The Washington Post reported, citing six people familiar with the plans, that the Pentagon is developing a bigger deep-cover intelligence program to wage economic warfare against China and other competitors, with operatives posing as businesspeople under non-government cover; former CIA officer Tom Rakusan is said to be overseeing the effort as a senior adviser to Deputy Defense Secretary Steve Feinberg. Congressional committees do not appear to have been briefed, according to the report, and Pentagon deputy press secretary Jacob Bliss denied it, saying the story "is simply not true." *Why it matters:* A Pentagon-run clandestine human-intelligence program focused on economic competition would blur lines with the CIA's traditional mission and raise oversight questions if confirmed.
([Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/national-security/2026/10/21/pentagon-looks-build-deep-cover-spy-program-confront-china/))

**Microsoft ties China-linked hackers to ConnectWise supply-chain breach reaching 2,250 organizations.** Microsoft attributed a supply-chain attack on IT software firm ConnectWise to Storm-0940, a China state-aligned hacking group, saying the attackers used an agentic AI penetration-testing framework to automate intrusions and reach more than 2,250 Microsoft 365 tenants at a rate of 2,900 sign-in attempts per minute. *Why it matters:* It's one of the clearer public examples of a state-linked group using AI tools to scale up a cyberespionage campaign.
([CyberScoop](https://cyberscoop.com/microsoft-attributes-connectwise-supply-chain-attack-china-storm-0940/))

**Russian hackers wiped data at a Ukrainian grain company in a joint operation.** Researchers said the Russian state-linked hacking group Sandworm collaborated with another threat actor to carry out a destructive cyberattack against a Ukrainian grain logistics company last year. *Why it matters:* It shows Russian military intelligence is still willing to pair with other hacking groups to hit Ukraine's agricultural export sector, a key part of its wartime economy.
([The Record](https://therecord.media/ukraine-hackers-russia-grain-sandworm))

**Haaretz investigation: fake accounts built to push Iran regime change now boost Netanyahu's campaign.** A Haaretz investigation found that fake social media accounts originally created to promote regime change in Iran have begun sharing Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's election campaign videos. *Why it matters:* It raises questions about covert influence networks built for foreign-facing messaging being repurposed for domestic Israeli politics.
([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/security-aviation/2026-10-22/ty-article-magazine/.premium/fake-accounts-used-against-irans-regime-now-amplify-netanyahus-attacks/000001a1-62bb-d64b-a1ff-7fffaa440000))

## US military and intelligence community

**NSA and FBI issue urgent warning over critical PHP flaws.** The NSA and FBI issued an urgent warning about critical flaws in PHP, the widely used website scripting language, saying organizations should immediately update to the latest version or take affected systems offline. *Why it matters:* PHP underpins a huge share of government and corporate websites, so unpatched systems create a wide, easily automated attack surface.
([Defense One](https://www.defenseone.com/threats/2026/10/critical-flaws-php-warning/416727/))

**GAO finds Pentagon's AI oversight office understaffed.** The Government Accountability Office found that the Pentagon office set up in 2022 to oversee the military's responsible use of artificial intelligence had filled only three of its 10 authorized positions as of February 2026. *Why it matters:* Thin staffing undercuts the Pentagon's ability to police how fast-moving AI tools are adopted across the department even as it pushes AI into more weapons and command systems.
([Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/news/pentagon-congress/2026/10/21/pentagon-ai-oversight-office-struggles-to-keep-pace-with-departments-ambitions-gao-finds/))

**Air Force tells Congress it needs 1,509 combat-ready fighters by 2035.** The Air Force told Congress it needs 1,509 combat-coded fighter jets by 2035, with F-35s making up 40% of the fleet and new F-47s replacing F-22s; a senior general also confirmed the service is weighing a new-build F-16 but said that decision isn't urgent. *Why it matters:* The target gives Congress a concrete benchmark to judge whether current fighter production and retirement plans will leave the Air Force short.
([Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/news/your-military/2026/10/21/us-air-force-outlines-the-fighter-fleet-it-needs-by-2035/), [Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/10/air-force-needs-1509-combat-ready-jets-by-2035-service-tells-congress/))

**Former officials ask full D.C. Circuit to revisit ruling upholding Pentagon's Anthropic ban.** A three-judge panel of the US Court of Appeals for the D.C. Circuit ruled 2-1 in September that the Pentagon could bar defense contractors from using Anthropic's Claude on military-related work, rejecting Anthropic's argument that the restriction violated its First Amendment rights; former US officials have now filed a brief urging the full court to reconsider, warning the ruling could threaten all defense contractors. *Why it matters:* The case could set a precedent for how much latitude the Pentagon has to blacklist AI vendors from contractor work without judicial pushback.
([DefenseScoop](https://defensescoop.com/2026/10/21/dc-circuit-decision-anthropic-pentagon-fight/))

## China, Russia, Iran and North Korea

**Russia says it's "ready in principle" to resume Ukraine peace talks after Witkoff pushes Putin, but shows little sign of compromise.** Russian President Vladimir Putin agreed to a US call to resume Ukraine peace talks after US envoy Steve Witkoff pressed him, Russia's government said, even as missiles hit Kyiv and Trump said the two sides are "the closest we've been" to ending the war. The BBC's Russia editor and Foreign Policy both reported Putin does not feel pressured to end the war and refused to compromise on key points in his talks with Witkoff. *Why it matters:* The gap between the diplomatic gesture and Moscow's actual position suggests the renewed talks are unlikely to produce a quick breakthrough.
([BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq3djn5nxx12o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss), [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cm98zn5j89kdo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss), [Foreign Policy](https://foreignpolicy.com/2026/10/21/witkoff-putin-russia-ukraine-peace-talks-zelensky-trump/))

**Iran's navy says it struck four vessels in the Gulf, with three more hitting mines, and warns the US of a wider response.** Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps Navy said it tracked and struck four vessels in the Persian Gulf and that three other vessels hit mines west of the Strait of Hormuz, and it warned Washington of a broader response if the US escalates further. *Why it matters:* An Iranian claim of hitting multiple vessels near the Strait of Hormuz raises the risk of disruption to a critical oil chokepoint, though Tehran's account hasn't been independently confirmed.
([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/middle-east-news/iran/2026-10-22/ty-article/iran-says-four-ships-caught-fire-in-gulf-three-others-hit-mines/000001a1-620f-d05a-abe9-671f63000000))

**Trump blocked a fresh Netanyahu push for pre-election strikes on Iran, Haaretz reports, as Israel and the US reportedly plan for a future campaign.** Haaretz reports Netanyahu pushed for another round of strikes on Iran before this week's election but Trump blocked the move, intensifying tension with the IDF over Netanyahu's use of the military in his campaign and pressure on the army chief. Separately, Haaretz cites a New York Times report that the US and Israel have drawn up plans for Iran strikes after the November US midterm elections, with both militaries stepping up exercises; Trump has not approved an attack. *Why it matters:* It shows Washington is still restraining Israeli action against Iran even as both countries keep contingency plans for a later strike on the table.
([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/elections/2026-10-21/ty-article/.premium/behind-netanyahus-failed-bid-for-a-pre-election-iran-war/000001a1-5f7c-d64b-a1ff-7f7eef9f0000), [Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/israel-security/2026-10-21/ty-article/u-s-and-israel-have-drawn-up-plans-for-iran-strikes-after-midterms-nyt-reports/000001a1-6122-da49-abe9-6ffa052f0000))

**Democratic lawmakers say Russia is working to undermine their party ahead of the midterms.** Two Democratic lawmakers said an intelligence update showed Russia is running a covert influence effort aimed at undermining their party ahead of the midterm elections, though they stressed no country is attempting the more technically difficult task of hacking into US election infrastructure or directly tampering with votes. *Why it matters:* It's a reminder that election interference efforts are continuing even without evidence of the harder-to-execute infrastructure attacks officials worry most about.
([The Record](https://therecord.media/democrat-lawmakers-say-russia-is-aiming-to-undermine-party))

## Germany and Israel

([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/israel-security/2026-10-21/ty-article/.premium/submarine-affair-probe-to-summon-netanyahu-idf-chief-for-closed-door-testimony/000001a1-5d77-d7ce-a9a1-dff776230000))

**IDF report: Hamas can only be dismantled through renewed fighting in Gaza.** An IDF intelligence assessment presented to US Army officers and Israeli officials concludes Hamas can only be dismantled through renewed fighting in Gaza, according to Haaretz, amid the Netanyahu government's reluctance to cooperate with the Gaza peace plan. *Why it matters:* The assessment points to a widening gap between the IDF's internal read on Hamas and the diplomatic track Israel is nominally still pursuing.
([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/israel-security/2026-10-22/ty-article/.premium/idf-report-asserts-hamas-can-only-be-dismantled-through-renewed-fighting-in-gaza/000001a1-6270-dd24-a1bf-6e7d35040000))

**Israeli officials warn of a settler violence "blitz" after the election.** Israeli security officials told Haaretz they expect a surge in settler violence in the West Bank after this week's election, and that the IDF, Shin Bet security service and police are struggling to contain the violence even as the military reinforces troops in the territory. *Why it matters:* It's an unusually direct admission from Israel's own security establishment that it may not be able to contain a feared spike in settler attacks.
([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/israel-security/2026-10-22/ty-article/.premium/israels-security-establishment-fears-surge-in-jewish-terror-after-election/000001a1-6075-da49-abe9-6ffda20f0000))
