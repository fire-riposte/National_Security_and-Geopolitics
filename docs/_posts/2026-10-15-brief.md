## Espionage and counterintelligence

**Hamburg court sentences Iran spy to eight and a half years over planned attacks on Jewish leaders.** The Hanseatic Higher Regional Court sentenced a Danish national of Afghan descent to eight and a half years in prison, and a co-defendant to four and a half years, for spying on behalf of Iran's Revolutionary Guards. Prosecutors said the main defendant scouted Central Council of Jews president Josef Schuster and German-Israeli Society president Volker Beck as targets for murder and arson attacks, and prepared a firebombing of a kosher shop in Berlin, acting on orders from the Quds Force; the verdicts are not yet final. *Why it matters:* The case shows Iran directing lethal plots against Jewish community figures on German soil, not just surveillance.
([ad-hoc-news.de, in German](https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/politik/iran-spion-in-hamburg-zu-achteinhalb-jahren-haft-verurteilt/70328749))

**Declassified FBI files detail Eric Swalwell's ties to suspected Chinese operative.** Newly declassified FBI records show former Rep. Eric Swalwell told agents in 2015 that he'd had a physical relationship on several occasions with Christine Fang, a Chinese national the FBI suspected of being an intelligence operative. Fang, also known as "Fang Fang," had raised funds for Swalwell's campaign and placed an intern in his congressional office before he cut off contact after the FBI warned him; he was never charged with wrongdoing. *Why it matters:* The files add detail to a case that has long illustrated how Chinese intelligence services cultivate ties to American officials' staff and donor networks.
([ABC News 4](https://abcnews4.com/news/nation-world/swalwell-and-fang-fang-declassified-fbi-files-reveal-physical-relationship-chinese-intelligence-congressional-office))

**Lithuania pushes EU to declare Russia's military intelligence a terrorist organization.** Lithuanian Foreign Minister Kęstutis Budrys called on the EU to designate Russia's GRU military intelligence agency as a terrorist organization, citing a recent arson attack in Munich and the August drone incident at Leipzig airport that Vilnius has blamed on Russian intelligence. Budrys said Lithuania could act alone if the EU doesn't, as it did before the bloc later designated Iran's Revolutionary Guards. *Why it matters:* A formal terrorist designation would give European governments sanctions and financial tools against GRU-linked operatives, not just diplomatic condemnation.
([Ukrainska Pravda](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2026/10/12/8057564/))

## US military and intelligence community

**FBI publicly debunks report that Director Patel was in Russia.** The FBI rebutted an MS NOW report, citing two unnamed sources, that Director Kash Patel had landed in Russia for meetings with its security service, posting video of Patel at a law enforcement event in Virginia the same day. MS NOW retracted the claim on air; Patel had been expected, per earlier Politico reporting, to make a tentative trip to Moscow and St. Petersburg this week. *Why it matters:* The episode shows how closely Patel's outside contacts with Russia are being watched, even as the planned trip itself remains unconfirmed.
([The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/media/6149007-fbi-refutes-ms-now-russia-report/), [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/photos-video-kash-patel-virginia-event-false-report-in-russia-12569583))

**Pentagon seeks $50 billion for mass drone production.** Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth said the Pentagon wants $50 billion in reconciliation funding to scale up autonomous and robotic systems. *Why it matters:* The request signals the Pentagon is betting on cheap, mass-produced drones as a core capability rather than a niche program.
([Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/news/pentagon-congress/2026/10/14/pentagon-needs-50b-for-mass-drone-production-hegseth-says/))

**Army issues new warfighting guidance built around four pillars.** The Army released "How The Army Fights," a 46-page guide built around disrupting, striking, assaulting and enduring, intended to shape how the service trains and fights on the modern battlefield. *Why it matters:* A unified doctrine document signals the Army is trying to standardize lessons from Ukraine and the Middle East across a force that has absorbed drones, long-range fires and contested logistics all at once.
([Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/news/your-military/2026/10/14/disrupt-strike-assault-and-endure-the-4-pillars-of-the-us-armys-new-warfare-guidance/))

## China, Russia, Iran and North Korea

**US has drawn up plans to hit Iran with "mother of all bombs," CNN reports.** CNN reported the US military has prepared strike options against Iranian nuclear and energy infrastructure, including the GBU-43/B "mother of all bombs," for use if Trump orders combat to resume. Sources familiar with internal military discussions told CNN that doubts remain over whether the weapon would meaningfully change the course of the war, and the report does not say Trump has made a decision. *Why it matters:* Planning for the bomb's first use against Iran shows the administration is preparing for a harder strike option, not just a resumption of the campaign already carried out.
([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/15/politics/us-considering-mother-of-all-bombs-iran))

**China and Pakistan hold first-ever joint army drill.** China's defense ministry said Chinese and Pakistani ground forces began "Karakoram 2026," their first joint army training exercise, in Xinjiang, featuring drone operations, long-range sniping and joint command drills focused on counterterrorism. The drill follows a string of attacks on Chinese nationals and projects in Pakistan. *Why it matters:* Extending China-Pakistan military cooperation from special-operations and naval exercises to regular ground forces signals deeper interoperability between the two militaries.
([The Print](https://theprint.in/world/china-pakistan-armies-to-hold-joint-drills/3068740/))

**China sanctions Paraguay and Belize officials over Taiwan ties.** Beijing imposed asset freezes and travel bans on Paraguay's communications minister and Belize's foreign minister, accusing them of visiting Taiwan and spreading "disinformation" about Chinese cyberattacks. Analysts called the sanctions largely symbolic since officials from Taiwan's remaining diplomatic allies rarely travel to China anyway. *Why it matters:* The sanctions are part of a pattern of Beijing punishing individual foreign officials, not just governments, to raise the cost of engaging with Taiwan.
([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/12/china-sanctions-paraguay-belize-ministers-over-taiwan-ties))

**Chinese carrier strike group drills in the Philippine Sea.** Japan's Joint Staff Office said the PLA Navy's Liaoning carrier strike group was operating in the Philippine Sea, spotted 155 miles south of Japan's Ishigaki Island conducting flight operations. *Why it matters:* The carrier's presence that close to Japanese territory underscores how routine Chinese naval operations near Japan's southwestern islands have become.
([USNI News](https://news.usni.org/2026/10/14/chinese-carrier-strike-group-drills-in-the-philippine-sea))

## Germany and Israel

**Bundeswehr investigates alleged "sex contest" among soldiers at a medical unit.** German prosecutors and the Bundeswehr are investigating roughly ten soldiers, including a platoon leader, at Medical Regiment 1 in Weissenfels over allegations that male soldiers competed over who could have sex with the most female recruits, with some women allegedly pressured into it; at least one alleged victim was reportedly a minor at the time. Defense Minister Boris Pistorius has promised consequences; nine suspects have been barred from barracks and several disciplinary cases referred to prosecutors. *Why it matters:* The case is the latest in a string of misconduct scandals that have forced the Bundeswehr's leadership to confront how abuse goes unreported in its ranks.
([t-online.de, in German](https://www.t-online.de/nachrichten/panorama/gesellschaft/id_101482122/bundeswehr-skandal-soldaten-sollen-sex-wettbewerb-veranstaltet-haben.html))

**Former Shin Bet chief Ronen Bar sues Netanyahu, detailing pre-October 7 warnings.** Ronen Bar filed his defamation suit against Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, laying out at least 20 warnings he says the Shin Bet gave Netanyahu's office about Gaza's security situation in the hours before Hamas's October 7 attack. Netanyahu responded that Bar "could have called him" directly if the threat was so serious; opposition leaders seized on the filing to renew calls for a state inquiry. *Why it matters:* The dueling accounts are becoming part of the public record through litigation, not just competing press statements, ahead of any formal state inquiry into October 7.
([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/israel-security/2026-10-14/ty-article/.premium/ex-shin-bet-head-suing-netanyahu-for-blood-libel-trying-to-shift-oct-7-blame/000001a1-391b-dd50-a1ad-3bfb9bf50000), [Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/israel-security/2026-10-14/ty-article/.premium/after-libel-suit-netanyahu-says-ex-shin-bet-chief-couldve-called-him-on-oct-7/000001a1-3a63-d72d-ada3-7b6ff48f0000))

**IDF and Shin Bet raid two West Bank charities they call Hamas and PFLP fronts.** Israeli forces raided the Ramallah offices of the legal-aid group Addameer and the Islamic Society for Orphans Welfare in Yatta, arresting five people and seizing computers and documents. The military said the groups funneled money to Hamas and the Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine; the groups' own accounts of the raids were not available in the reporting. *Why it matters:* Treating legal-aid and charity groups as terror financing fronts broadens the scope of Israel's counterterror operations in the West Bank beyond armed cells.
([Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/idf-shin-bet-raid-two-west-bank-charities-they-say-were-fronts-for-terror-groups/), [Ynetnews](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/r4wez2bhv))

**German air force fires IRIS-T SLM live for the first time.** The Luftwaffe conducted its first live firing of the IRIS-T SLM air-defense system on a NATO range in Crete, two years after the system was handed to the Bundeswehr, alongside Swedish forces and the German army testing the system jointly. *Why it matters:* The live-fire test comes as Germany races to field more layered air defense amid repeated Russian drone incursions into NATO airspace.
([Augen geradeaus!, in German](https://augengeradeaus.net/2026/10/zwei-jahre-nach-uebergabe-an-die-bundeswehr-erster-scharfer-schuss-der-luftwaffe-mit-iris-t-slm/))
