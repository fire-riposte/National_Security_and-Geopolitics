**Bottom line:** Russian military hackers breached defense contractors in two NATO countries, the latest sign Moscow is stepping up espionage against the alliance's defense industry. Germany sentenced two men for spying for Iran in connection with a plot targeting Jewish community leaders and summoned Tehran's ambassador, while US Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth made an unannounced trip to Israel as the IDF tightened security ahead of the October 27 Knesset election. In Washington, bipartisan senators are pressing Trump to reverse a deal allowing Russian diesel imports.

## Espionage and counterintelligence

**Russian hackers breach NATO defense contractors.** The group tracked as APT28, or Fancy Bear and linked to Russian military intelligence, used a newly documented backdoor to maintain access inside the networks of two defense contractors and a law enforcement agency in two NATO member countries, according to researchers. *Why it matters:* It is a reminder that Russian military intelligence keeps targeting the Western defense industrial base that supplies Ukraine, even as diplomacy over the war continues.
([The Record](https://therecord.media/european-defense-contractors-fancy-bear-espionage))

**Germany sentences two men for spying for Iran in plot against Jewish leaders, summons ambassador.** A German court sentenced two men to nearly a decade in prison for espionage in connection with an alleged Iranian-directed plot targeting leaders of Jewish organizations in Germany. Berlin's Foreign Ministry summoned Iran's ambassador over the case days after the sentencing. *Why it matters:* It is the latest in a run of German cases tying Iranian intelligence to surveillance and plots against Jewish and Israeli targets in Europe.
([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/world-news/europe/2026-10-19/ty-article/.premium/germany-summons-iranian-envoy-over-plot-to-kill-leaders-of-jewish-organizations/000001a1-5401-d5aa-a9e5-dda743780000))

**Saudi national charged with planting recording device at OpenAI.** Federal prosecutors charged Abdulrahman Alfozan, a Saudi national and former OpenAI engineer, with secretly installing an audio recording device, concealed in toilet paper, behind a television in a conference room used for executive meetings. He was arrested on October 9. FBI Director Kash Patel said protecting the company's advanced AI research from espionage is now a top counterintelligence priority; one outlet has reported, without confirmation, that Alfozan may have been acting for the Saudi government, but prosecutors have not disclosed a motive. *Why it matters:* The case shows leading AI labs are now a direct espionage target, not just a cybersecurity one.
([Colorado Politics](https://www.coloradopolitics.com/2026/10/15/fbi-arrests-saudi-openai-employee-accused-of-bugging-office/))

## US military and intelligence community

**Senator warns hundreds of federal websites lack basic protection against spies.** A US senator said hundreds of federal government websites, including sites for FBI tips, intelligence job applications and the Veterans Crisis Line, lack encryption protections that would guard against surveillance of visitors. *Why it matters:* Unencrypted connections to sensitive government sites could let adversary intelligence services identify people reporting tips or applying for intelligence jobs.
([Defense One](https://www.defenseone.com/threats/2026/10/senator-warns-hundreds-federal-web-addresses-may-lack-key-spying-protection/416671/))

**Pentagon threatens university funding over ties to "foreign entities of concern."** The Pentagon warned research universities that partnerships with organizations on its list of foreign entities of concern put their federal funding at risk and hinted at additional compliance audits. *Why it matters:* It extends a broader US push to cut adversary access to federally funded research.
([DefenseScoop](https://defensescoop.com/2026/10/19/pentagon-research-security-audits-universities/))

**Marine Corps stands up its first anti-ship missile battalion.** The Marine Corps redesignated the 1st Battalion, 10th Marine Regiment, part of the 2nd Marine Division, as its first dedicated medium-range missile battalion, equipped with the NMESIS anti-ship missile system, during a ceremony at Camp Lejeune. *Why it matters:* It creates a dedicated unit built around a weapon the Marine Corps has prioritized for a potential fight with China in the Pacific.
([USNI News](https://news.usni.org/2026/10/19/u-s-marine-corps-stands-up-first-nmesis-anti-ship-missile-battalion), [Department of War](https://www.war.gov/News/News-Stories/Article/Article/4628693/marine-corps-battalion-redesignates-as-first-medium-missile-battalion/))

## China, Russia, Iran and North Korea

**Russian satellite shadows Ukraine-linked radar satellite in unusual maneuver.** Russia's Kosmos 2612 satellite came within about 500 meters of ICEYE-X36, a US-flagged radar-imaging satellite believed to support Ukraine, after weeks of unusual maneuvering, according to tracking firm LeoLabs. *Why it matters:* Close approaches between military and commercial satellites raise the risk of miscalculation in an increasingly contested orbital environment tied to the war in Ukraine.
([The War Zone](https://www.twz.com/news-features/russias-satellite-unprecedented-shadowing-of-a-very-particular-radar-imaging-satellite-is-a-big-deal), [Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/10/russian-satellite-stalked-iceye-spacecraft-in-potentially-dangerous-on-orbit-maneuvers/))

**Bipartisan senators demand Trump reverse Russian diesel deal as rift with Zelenskyy deepens.** A bipartisan group of roughly a dozen US senators, including Republicans John Cornyn and Mitch McConnell, demanded Trump rescind his deal easing sanctions to allow Russian diesel imports, arguing it violates a 2022 law banning Russian energy purchases; Representative Brian Fitzpatrick said he would introduce legislation to block all Russian oil purchases. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has called the deal "a gift to Putin," and Trump has responded by suggesting Ukraine needs new leadership and accusing Zelenskyy of defying his order not to strike Russian refineries. *Why it matters:* Bipartisan pushback in Congress, layered on a deepening rift with Kyiv, signals growing resistance to how far the administration will go to ease pressure on Moscow's energy exports while the war continues.
([Alaska's News Source](https://www.alaskasnewssource.com/2026/10/14/murkowski-bipartisan-senators-urge-trump-reverse-russian-diesel-deal-president-rallies-wasilla/), [ITV News](https://www.itv.com/news/2026-10-09/trump-says-us-to-obtain-diesel-from-russia-despite-energy-sanctions))

## Germany and Israel

**Hegseth makes unannounced visit to Israel, meets Netanyahu and Katz.** US Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth made an unannounced visit to Israel, meeting Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and Defense Minister Israel Katz. The Pentagon did not announce the trip in advance, and US officials told reporters it had not been planned. *Why it matters:* An unannounced visit by the US defense chief, arriving as Israel holds a sensitive election, points to fast-moving coordination between the two governments.
([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/elections/2026-10-20/ty-article/u-s-defense-secretary-hegseth-meets-netanyahu-katz-in-unannounced-israel-visit/000001a1-57e3-df3c-adbf-d7f363890000))

**IDF suspends soldiers' leave, reinforces West Bank presence ahead of Knesset election.** Israel's military suspended soldiers' leave and reinforced its West Bank presence ahead of the October 27 Knesset election; soldier voting for those serving overseas or in isolated postings began Tuesday. Military sources told Haaretz they have not identified a specific threat but are concerned actors could try to exploit heightened tensions around the vote. *Why it matters:* The preventive posture shows Israeli security services treat the election period itself as a potential flashpoint, even without a concrete threat.
([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/israel-politics/2026-10-19/ty-article/.premium/idf-suspends-soldiers-leave-ahead-of-election-reinforces-presence-in-west-bank/000001a1-5487-d2a4-a3e3-75bf3a430000), [Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/israel-politics/2026-10-19/ty-article/.premium/idf-sources-no-specific-threat-was-identified-on-the-eve-of-the-election/000001a1-552f-df3c-adbf-d57f59690001))
