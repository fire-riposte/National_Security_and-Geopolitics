## Espionage and counterintelligence

**Andhra Pradesh police arrest two more in widening Pakistan espionage probe.** Police arrested Pradeep Mukharjee, a civilian employee at the Indian Navy's Visakhapatnam base, and Annam Sai Varaprasad, a driver for naval officers, accusing them of passing classified naval and defense information to Pakistani intelligence operatives for money. The arrests are part of "Operation Firewall-2026," a Counter Intelligence Cell investigation spanning 10 states that now covers 18 suspects, 13 of them Navy personnel. *Why it matters:* The scale of the alleged network, more than a dozen Navy personnel implicated, points to a sustained recruitment effort rather than an isolated leak.
([The Federal](https://thefederal.com/category/states/south/andhra-pradesh/andhra-arrest-two-alleged-pakistan-spy-case-13-navy-personnel-18-suspect-258743), [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/india/story/2-arrested-in-andhra-for-spying-for-pakistan-including-naval-staffer-559965-2026-10-06))

**Newly released FBI records detail Eric Swalwell's ties to suspected Chinese operative.** Newly disclosed FBI records describe a 2013-2014 investigation into then-Representative Eric Swalwell's relationship with Christine Fang, a suspected Chinese intelligence operative who also helped place an intern in his office and assisted with his fundraising. The FBI briefed Swalwell on its concerns in 2015; he says he cut off contact with Fang afterward and was never charged. *Why it matters:* The records resurface one of the most prominent examples of a suspected Chinese influence operation reaching into a sitting member of Congress's office.
([KUTV](https://kutv.com/news/nation-world/eric-swalwell-fbi-records-christine-fang-house-intelligence-committee-chinese-operative-mccarthy))

## China, Russia, Iran and North Korea

**UK blames Iran's Revolutionary Guard for plot against RAF Fairford, vows new sanctions.** Prime Minister Andy Burnham said it was "highly likely" that Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps coordinated a suspected plot targeting RAF Fairford, a British air base, and pledged new sanctions against Tehran. Iran denied involvement, calling the allegations "unfounded and malicious speculations." *Why it matters:* A direct UK accusation against the IRGC over a plot on British soil marks a sharp escalation in London's confrontation with Iran.
([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/world-news/europe/2026-10-12/ty-article/u-k-pm-burnham-irans-irgc-likely-behind-raf-fairford-incident/000001a1-30d4-dbe9-ade9-72deccca0000))

**Drones sink two ships in Bulgarian waters, prime minister summons Russian ambassador.** Bulgaria's prime minister said two merchant ships were hit by drones in Bulgarian waters and that he suspects Russia was behind the attacks; he summoned Russia's ambassador over the incident. *Why it matters:* A direct drone attack on shipping in a NATO and EU member's waters would be one of the most serious escalations of Russian hybrid activity against the alliance to date.
([BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmn06w5x9g25o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss))

**Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia keep escalating, as Netanyahu pushes to join the fight.** Houthi strikes on Saudi Arabia, which have already killed more than a dozen people this month, are forcing Trump to weigh whether to help Riyadh counter the attacks without reigniting the US war with Yemen he sought to end, Haaretz reported. Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu is separately pushing to join Saudi Arabia's fight against the Houthis as a step toward normalization, though a Haaretz source says Saudi Arabia's crown prince opposes the move. *Why it matters:* A direct Israeli or US role fighting the Houthis alongside Saudi Arabia would widen the conflict and tie it more closely to the broader confrontation with Iran.
([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/middle-east-news/2026-10-13/ty-article/.premium/trumps-trap-how-a-saudi-lifeline-could-ignite-fresh-war-with-iran/000001a1-3234-dd6b-aff3-fe7742870000), [Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/middle-east-news/2026-10-12/ty-article/.premium/netanyahu-seeks-to-join-saudis-fight-against-houthis-crown-prince-opposes-move/000001a1-3117-d20b-a1ff-f557b0810000))

**Russia's offensive capability is "broken," Ukraine's outgoing top general says.** General Oleksandr Syrskyi, forced from his post as Ukraine's top general, told the BBC in his first interview since leaving that Russia's offensive ability is broken. The comments come as Ukraine says Russia keeps striking its energy grid despite talk of a partial ceasefire, with six energy sites hit since Trump announced the energy truce. *Why it matters:* A public assessment from Ukraine's own former top general that Russia's offensive is spent cuts against Moscow's narrative of momentum, even as strikes on civilian infrastructure continue.
([BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c9y7lgg2vdggo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss), [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cvlyd4pdlwkxo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss))

**Ukraine strikes third Yandex data center, disrupting Russian cloud services.** Ukrainian drones hit a third Yandex data center in Russia over the weekend, forcing a shutdown that disrupted cloud, payment and delivery services across the country. *Why it matters:* The repeated strikes show Ukraine is willing to target Russian civilian-facing digital infrastructure, not just military and energy sites.
([The Record](https://therecord.media/ukraine-strikes-third-yandex-data-center))

## Germany and Israel

**Shin Bet joins UAE probe into flydubai co-pilot accused of trying to crash jet, cites "revenge for Gaza" motive.** Israeli Shin Bet officers flew to the UAE to take part in interrogating Hamam al-Hammami, the Omani co-pilot accused of attempting to hijack and crash flydubai Flight FZ1073 on September 30. Israeli officials, citing the co-pilot's own statements as relayed by Channel 12, say he described the plot as revenge for Gaza and initially planned to crash the aircraft into Ben Gurion Airport before having a backup plan targeting a US base in Jordan; UAE authorities have called it an attempted terrorist attack, and investigators currently believe he acted alone. *Why it matters:* Israeli intelligence's direct role in interrogating a suspect in another country's custody shows how closely Shin Bet is tracking plots it sees as Gaza-war blowback, even against targets with no direct Israeli link.
([Ynetnews](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/hkq3g00bjfe))

**IDF prepares more than 100 extra polling stations in Gaza, Lebanon and Syria for Israel's election.** The IDF is deploying forces to roughly 100 more military polling stations than in the previous election, including sites in Gaza, Lebanon and Syria, and has warned commanders against making political statements within their units. *Why it matters:* The scale of the deployment reflects how much Israel's military footprint outside its borders has grown since the last election.
([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/elections/2026-10-12/ty-article/.premium/with-polling-stations-in-gaza-lebanon-and-syria-idf-prepares-for-election/000001a1-3008-db9b-adf9-f8e8f33f0000))
