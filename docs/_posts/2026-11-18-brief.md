**Bottom line:** The UK transferred US Army Lieutenant Colonel Brandon Tyner to American military custody over allegations he spied for Russia, and Israeli media say Mossad helped foil an Iran-backed plot to assassinate an Israeli businessman in Greece. Iran kept striking US and allied sites in the Gulf as Trump heads into the first US-China-Russia summit in Shenzhen pushing nuclear arms cuts, while Polish officials say they broke up a Russian plan for a mass-casualty attack meant to be blamed on Ukraine.

## Espionage and counterintelligence

**UK hands Army officer accused of spying for Russia over to US military.** Britain transferred Lieutenant Colonel Brandon Tyner, a senior human-resources officer for NATO's Allied Rapid Reaction Corps who was arrested near Gloucester on November 10, into US military custody, clearing the way for prosecution under the Uniform Code of Military Justice. US officials have said no protected information is believed to have reached Moscow, and the Kremlin has denied any connection to Tyner. *Why it matters:* an alleged Russian recruitment effort against an officer at a NATO headquarters is now moving into a US court-martial, the next real test of what prosecutors can prove. ([BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6x284ez38vdo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss))

**Mossad reportedly helped foil Iran-linked assassination plot in Greece.** Greek and North Macedonian authorities arrested five people over an alleged plot to assassinate an Israeli businessman in Greece, which they say was orchestrated by an Iranian national tied to Tehran's security services and an Iranian crime boss. Israeli media, citing foreign reports, said Mossad helped uncover the plot; Israeli officials have not confirmed the agency's role. *Why it matters:* it's the latest sign Iran is still running assassination plots against Israeli targets in Europe despite the broader war's diplomatic track. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/world-news/europe/2026-11-17/ty-article/north-macedonia-greece-say-they-foiled-iran-linked-assassination-plot/000001a1-ead0-dc88-a1a7-feda25720000))

**Pakistani national recruited by Iran's IRGC sentenced to 27 years for plot to kill US officials.** A federal judge in Brooklyn sentenced Asif Merchant, 48, to 330 months in prison after a jury convicted him of trying to hire hitmen, who turned out to be undercover FBI agents, to assassinate US officials including President Trump. Prosecutors said Merchant was recruited by Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps around 2022 and was seeking revenge for the 2020 US drone strike that killed IRGC commander Qassem Soleimani. *Why it matters:* it's one of the clearest US convictions yet of an Iranian-directed plot to kill a sitting president. ([Justice Department](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/iranian-intelligence-agent-sentenced-over-27-years-prison-foiled-plot-assassinate-us))

**Pakistani hackers targeted defense workers through fake job sites.** Researchers said Pakistani hacking groups built fake job-recruitment sites to spy on job seekers, mostly in India, with some targeting that may extend to defense contractors and military personnel. *Why it matters:* recruitment scams are becoming a low-cost way for state-linked hackers to get inside defense-adjacent targets. ([CyberScoop](https://cyberscoop.com/pakistani-hackers-defense-workers-fake-job-sites/))

## US military and intelligence community

**Classified NSA network goes down.** A communications network used by the NSA, NSANet, went offline, and people familiar with the matter said there are no immediate signs the outage is related to a cyberattack; it may instead be tied to a round of software upgrades. *Why it matters:* even a routine outage on a classified network used across the intelligence community draws scrutiny given recent tech failures elsewhere in government. ([Defense One](https://www.defenseone.com/threats/2026/11/classified-nsa-communications-network-down-people-familiar-say/417278/))

**Trump nominates LaNeve as Army chief of staff.** The White House formally nominated Gen. Christopher LaNeve to be the Army's next chief of staff, and separately nominated Lieutenant General Marcus Evans to fill LaNeve's current post as vice chief. *Why it matters:* it fills out the Army's top leadership at a time when the service is managing readiness strain from Middle East deployments and ongoing modernization pushes. ([Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/news/pentagon-congress/2026/11/17/laneve-formally-nominated-for-army-chief-of-staff-role/), [Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/11/white-house-officially-nominates-laneve-for-army-chief/))

**Pentagon fails ninth consecutive financial audit.** An inspector general report found the Defense Department failed its financial audit for a ninth straight year, though an official said a majority of its units passed for the first time. *Why it matters:* the Pentagon still can't fully account for how it spends its budget, even as that budget keeps growing. ([Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/pentagon/2026/11/17/pentagon-fails-ninth-straight-audit-despite-progress-in-organizing-finances/))

## China, Russia, Iran and North Korea

**Trump heads into first US-China-Russia summit pushing nuclear arms cuts.** Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin are set to meet in Shenzhen for the first-ever trilateral summit among the three leaders, with the Trump administration pushing to discuss nuclear arms reductions even though analysts say the odds of a real breakthrough are low. *Why it matters:* it's the first time the three nuclear powers' leaders have sat down together, at a moment when arms control between them has largely stalled. ([War on the Rocks](https://warontherocks.com/the-u-s-chinese-russian-summit-and-the-future-of-trilateral-nuclear-talks/), [Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/global/the-americas/2026/11/17/trump-seeks-nuclear-cuts-at-summit-with-putin-and-xi-but-prospects-are-low/))

**Iran strikes US-linked radar site in Bahrain despite ceasefire talks.** Iran's Revolutionary Guards said they targeted a US military radar system in Bahrain, warning Manama over its alliance with Washington, even as Qatari and Iranian officials held talks aimed at ending the nine-month war and attacks on shipping in the Strait of Hormuz keep escalating. *Why it matters:* Iran is still testing Gulf air defenses while a diplomatic track toward ending the war is supposedly underway. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/middle-east-news/iran/2026-11-17/ty-article/iran-attacks-u-s-military-radar-system-in-bahrain-despite-talks-with-mediators/000001a1-e9e9-d848-abeb-fbe924550000))

**Poland says it broke up a Russian plan to stage a mass-casualty attack and blame Ukraine.** Polish daily Rzeczpospolita reported, citing unnamed sources, that Russian intelligence services planned a sabotage operation near the Kaliningrad border in late October or early November meant to be blamed on Ukraine and aimed at halting Western support for Kyiv; one source compared it to the 1939 Gleiwitz incident Nazi Germany staged to justify invading Poland. Poland's interior minister did not publicly confirm the mass-casualty detail, and the Polish government has not corroborated the anonymous sourcing behind the report. *Why it matters:* it fits a documented pattern of Russian sabotage and false-flag planning against Poland. ([Militarnyi](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/poland-foils-russian-plot-to-cause-mass-casualties-and-frame-ukraine/))

**Suspected Russian aircraft violate Japanese airspace twice.** Japan's defense minister said suspected Russian aircraft violated Japanese territorial airspace twice, prompting fighter jets to scramble in response. *Why it matters:* it's part of a recent run of Russian air activity near Japan and Taiwan that's kept regional air forces on alert. ([USNI News](https://news.usni.org/2026/11/17/suspected-russian-aircraft-violated-japans-airspace-says-defense-minister))

## Germany and Israel

**Bundeswehr to study Ukraine's DELTA battlefield system for possible adoption.** Defense Minister Boris Pistorius said Germany will closely study Ukraine's DELTA digital battlefield-management platform, which fuses drone, sensor and unit data into real-time situational awareness, as part of a broader memorandum signed in Kyiv covering combat-data exchange and joint drone development. *Why it matters:* Germany is looking to import battlefield software shaped by years of Russian fighting rather than build its own from scratch. ([United24 Media](https://united24media.com/war-in-ukraine/berlin-eyes-ukraines-delta-battlefield-network-while-bundeswehr-studies-ukrainian-combat-tactics-18675))

**Bundeswehr troop count hits new high; Pistorius says no draft needed for now.** Germany's defense ministry said active-duty Bundeswehr personnel rose by about 4,400 in October to a new record of roughly 190,600 soldiers, and Pistorius said the growth means Germany doesn't foresee needing to reinstate mandatory conscription. *Why it matters:* Berlin's recruitment push keeps outperforming expectations, at least for now easing pressure to bring back the draft. ([Augen geradeaus!, in German](https://augengeradeaus.net/2026/11/personalstaerke-oktober-2026-rekordzunahme-auf-190-600-soldaten-brauchen-absehbar-keine-wehrpflicht/))

**Israel's elections chief says Shin Bet probe has yet to find evidence of vote-buying.** Central Elections Committee chairman Noam Sohlberg said the Shin Bet-police investigation into suspected foreign funding and election bribery has not yet produced concrete evidence, even as he defended Shin Bet chief David Zini against criticism over the ongoing probe. *Why it matters:* a high-profile domestic-intelligence investigation into Israel's own election is still open months later without the evidence its backers said justified it. ([Ynetnews](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/b1xurp70gg))
