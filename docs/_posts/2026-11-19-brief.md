**Bottom line:** US authorities charged or arrested two alleged Russian spies this week, a Silicon Valley defense-contractor target and a US Army lieutenant colonel handed over by the UK, while Estonia moved to close its border with Russia over a foiled GRU sabotage attempt on a drone maker. Israel pressed Washington for renewed strikes on Iran as its military and intelligence chiefs met the top US Central Command general in Tel Aviv, and a classified NSA network outage disrupted intelligence support in the Middle East as the Iran war continues.

## Espionage and counterintelligence

**Russian national indicted for spying on Palantir, stalking Silicon Valley workers.** A federal grand jury indicted Igor D'iu, a Russian national, on charges of acting as an agent of the Russian government without notifying the Attorney General, interstate stalking, and identity document fraud, the Justice Department said. Prosecutors allege D'iu surveilled and targeted employees of a Silicon Valley defense and technology company that assists Ukraine, which multiple outlets identified as Palantir Technologies. *Why it matters:* it is the latest case of Russian intelligence directly targeting US defense and tech firms tied to Ukraine's war effort. ([Justice Department](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/russian-national-indicted-acting-foreign-agent-russia-target-silicon-valley-technology-and), [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq20xg80ly47o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss))

**UK hands US Army officer accused of spying for Russia to US military.** British Counter Terrorism Policing transferred Lieutenant Colonel Brandon Tyner, arrested near Gloucester on November 10, to US custody on November 17 over suspected espionage and national security offenses. Tyner, who was stationed at NATO's Allied Rapid Reaction Corps headquarters in England, has not been charged; a US official told the Associated Press he is not believed to have passed information to Russia, and the Kremlin denied any link to him. *Why it matters:* a serving US officer with access to a NATO command under investigation for spying raises fresh questions about vetting inside alliance headquarters. ([ITV News](https://www.itv.com/news/westcountry/2026-11-17/us-soldier-arrested-on-suspicion-of-spying-for-russia-handed-over), [Kiro7](https://www.kiro7.com/news/politics/us-military-takes/LYN2PQJZZE2UZMG6XFY5WJPK7Y/))

**Estonia to close Russia border after accusing GRU of sabotage plot.** Estonia's foreign minister said the country will shut its last border crossings with Russia, Narva, Luhamaa and Koidula, from December 1, after security services blamed Russia's military intelligence agency (GRU) for a foiled sabotage attempt against Threod Systems, an Estonian drone maker. The Kremlin called the accusation baseless. *Why it matters:* it is the second sabotage attempt this year targeting an Estonian drone manufacturer, part of a wider pattern of suspected Russian sabotage against defense firms on NATO's eastern flank. ([Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/11/16/estonia-to-close-russia-border-after-sabotage-bid-on-drone-maker-foreign-minister-says), [Newsweek](https://newsweek.com/nato-member-estonia-to-shut-russia-border-after-sabotage-attack-12717506))

**Mossad reportedly helped Greece and North Macedonia foil Iran-linked assassination plot.** North Macedonia's security agency said it arrested four North Macedonian citizens, plus a fifth returned from Greece, over a plot to kill a business executive at an Israeli company in Athens, allegedly run by an Iranian national tied to Tehran's security services and an EU-sanctioned trafficking network. Ynet, citing sources, reported Mossad shared intelligence with Greek and North Macedonian authorities that helped uncover the plot; Israeli officials have not confirmed this. *Why it matters:* it is the latest in a string of Iran-linked plots against Israeli-connected targets in Europe. ([Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/11/17/north-macedonia-prevents-terrorist-assassination-plot-targeting-person-in-greece), [Ynetnews](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/h3fpbw8cw))

**Russian state hackers use stolen cloud credentials to impersonate contacts.** Google's threat researchers said Russian state-backed hackers are using information stolen from compromised cloud accounts to impersonate trusted contacts and launch new espionage intrusions against people in their victims' professional networks. *Why it matters:* it shows Russian cyber-espionage units adapting stolen cloud data into more convincing social-engineering lures. ([The Record](https://therecord.media/russia-cyber-espionage-google))

## US military and intelligence community

**NSA outage disrupts intelligence support during Iran war.** NSANet, the agency's classified internal network, has had disruptions for several days that hit a Georgia-based hub supporting Middle East intelligence operations during the US campaign against Iran, The Record and Defense One reported. Officials said there is no sign the outage is linked to a cyberattack and it may stem from software upgrades, though classified work has continued. *Why it matters:* an extended outage at a hub supporting an active war shows how fragile intelligence infrastructure can strain military operations. ([The Record](https://therecord.media/nsa-continues-response-to-nsa-net-problems), [Defense One](https://www.defenseone.com/threats/2026/11/classified-nsa-communications-network-down-people-familiar-say/417278/))

**Senate grills acting Navy secretary over Iran war costs.** Acting Navy Secretary Hung Cao told the Senate Armed Services Committee during his confirmation hearing that the Navy is recoding and firing off expired munitions during the Iran war, and faced sharp questioning from Democrats over extended deployments and his description of failed suicide attempts among sailors as "mitigations." *Why it matters:* the hearing exposed how the continuing Iran war is straining Navy readiness and munitions stocks. ([Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/newsletters/daily-news-roundup/2026/11/18/us-navy-used-expired-munitions-during-iran-war-cao-says/), [Defense One](https://www.defenseone.com/defense-systems/2026/11/senate-grills-acting-navy-secretary-iran-war-costs-extended-deployments/417307/))

**Senate appropriators cut Navy shipbuilding and Golden Dome funding as Pentagon fails ninth straight audit.** Senate appropriators cut nearly $20 billion from the Navy's shipbuilding request and slashed the roughly $17 billion sought for the Golden Dome missile-defense program in a draft 2027 defense bill, days after the Pentagon failed its ninth consecutive financial audit; the department's own oversight office ranked eliminating fraud among its top strategic challenges. *Why it matters:* Congress is pushing back on major Pentagon programs just as the department's books remain in disarray. ([USNI News](https://news.usni.org/2026/11/18/senate-appropriators-cut-20b-from-navy-shipbuilding-request), [Defense One](https://www.defenseone.com/policy/2026/11/appropriators-slash-trumps-17b-golden-dome-request-draft-bill/417326/), [Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/news/pentagon-congress/2026/11/18/watchdog-flags-fraud-failed-pentagon-audit-as-defense-priorities/))

**Pentagon revives "Office of Strategic Services" name for special operations post.** Secretary of War Pete Hegseth gave the Pentagon's top special operations civilian post the name of World War Two's Office of Strategic Services, the historic forerunner of the CIA, the Cipher Brief reported, though what new authorities come with the name change remains unclear. *Why it matters:* reviving the name raises questions about overlap with the CIA, which inherited the original agency's mission and legal authorities. ([The Cipher Brief](https://www.thecipherbrief.com/bluf-the-oss-is-back-in-name-the-hard-part-is-the-authorities))

## China, Russia, Iran and North Korea

**Israel presses US for renewed strikes on Iran as CENTCOM chief visits Tel Aviv.** US Central Command chief Gen. Cooper met Israeli military chief Eyal Zamir and Mossad chief Gofman in Tel Aviv amid reports Israel is seeking US backing for a new round of strikes on Iran, Haaretz reported. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu is separately pressing President Trump for a four-to-seven day joint campaign targeting Iranian nuclear and energy sites, according to Israeli officials cited by Haaretz. *Why it matters:* a renewed joint US-Israeli campaign would mark a major escalation months after the two countries already fought a war against Iran this year. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/israel-security/2026-11-18/ty-article/.premium/u-s-israeli-military-chiefs-meet-as-israel-pushes-for-new-iran-strikes/000001a1-ee98-ddcb-a9b3-febfbe610000), [Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/israel-security/2026-11-19/ty-article/report-netanyahu-presses-trump-for-four-to-seven-day-campaign-against-iran/000001a1-f1b2-d9ff-a7b5-f1bec1d70000))

**Iran widens attacks on US-aligned Gulf states amid stalled ceasefire talks.** Iran's Revolutionary Guards renewed strikes on US military targets and a radar system in Kuwait and Bahrain even as Qatari and Iranian officials continued talks aimed at ending the war, The War Zone and Haaretz reported. *Why it matters:* Iran is combining battlefield attacks with diplomacy, raising the risk that fighting with Gulf states hosting US forces could widen before any truce takes hold. ([The War Zone](https://www.twz.com/news-features/tehran-widens-attacks-on-gulf-nations-after-latest-u-s-strike-on-iranian-island), [Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/middle-east-news/iran/2026-11-17/ty-article/iran-attacks-u-s-military-radar-system-in-bahrain-despite-talks-with-mediators/000001a1-e9e9-d848-abeb-fbe924550000))

**Russian bombers land in Vietnam after airspace violations near Japan.** Two Russian Tu-95 bombers and two Il-76 transport planes landed in Da Nang, Vietnam, after suspected Russian aircraft violated Japanese airspace twice and approached Taiwan, prompting Japan to scramble fighters, The War Zone and USNI News reported. *Why it matters:* the flights show Russia projecting airpower into Southeast Asia while probing Japanese air defenses amid rising Indo-Pacific tension. ([The War Zone](https://www.twz.com/air/russian-bombers-arrive-in-vietnam-after-flurry-of-northeast-asia-air-alerts), [USNI News](https://news.usni.org/2026/11/17/suspected-russian-aircraft-violated-japans-airspace-says-defense-minister))

## Germany and Israel

**German military bars Chinese-made drones and restricts Chinese cars over spying fears.** Der Spiegel reported the Bundeswehr has banned commercial Chinese drones, mainly DJI models, from barracks and training grounds and ordered existing devices grounded, after Germany's military counterintelligence service reportedly found evidence that drones had exfiltrated data; soldiers who own Chinese-brand cars such as BYD, Geely and NIO must now park them outside military sites. *Why it matters:* the reported findings suggest Chinese commercial hardware may already have leaked data on Bundeswehr exercises and weapons, pushing Germany to follow Poland in restricting Chinese vehicles at military sites. ([United24 Media](https://united24media.com/world/germany-bans-chinese-drones-and-cars-from-military-bases-over-spying-fears-24261), [Kuwait Times](https://kuwaittimes.com/article/53434/world/german-military-bans-chinese-made-drones-over-security-concerns/))

**IDF says it completed Gaza ground operation beyond the "Yellow Line."** The Israeli military said it finished a ground operation to dismantle what it called a "terror tunnel" west of Gaza's ceasefire demarcation line; Hamas called the incursion a "flagrant" ceasefire violation, the BBC reported. *Why it matters:* the operation tests how far Israel will go in enforcing the ceasefire line without the deal collapsing. ([BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cw8r7510degno?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss))

**Bundeswehr personnel hits new record; no draft needed for now, Pistorius says.** Germany's active-duty military strength rose by about 4,400 in October to a new record of more than 190,600 soldiers, Defense Minister Boris Pistorius said, adding that Germany does not foreseeably need to reinstate conscription. *Why it matters:* Pistorius is citing the recruiting numbers to argue Germany's voluntary buildup can meet NATO's expanded force targets without a draft. ([Augen geradeaus!, in German](https://augengeradeaus.net/2026/11/personalstaerke-oktober-2026-rekordzunahme-auf-190-600-soldaten-brauchen-absehbar-keine-wehrpflicht/))
