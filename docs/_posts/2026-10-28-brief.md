**Bottom line:** A Geneva-based satellite-tracking firm cited by the Associated Press (AP) found Russian military satellites massing over Saudi targets just before Iran-backed strikes hit them this year, and Iran International reported that Iran's intelligence service engineered an oil minister's removal to protect a sanctioned oil-sales network routed through Russia. ## Espionage and counterintelligence

**Hackers repeatedly breached third-party suppliers holding UK police data.** His Majesty's Inspectorate of Constabulary and Fire & Rescue Services found British police data has been breached several times this decade through outside information-technology suppliers. *Why it matters:* Police forces increasingly outsource data handling, and each supplier is a separate point hackers can hit instead of the police themselves.
([The Record](https://therecord.media/uk-police-data-breached-from-suppliers-watchdog-report))

**A Ukrainian national was sentenced to nearly six years for ransomware attacks on hospitals and governments worldwide.** The Justice Department says Artem Stryzhak, 36, was sentenced to 70 months in prison for conspiracy to commit computer fraud tied to the Nefilim and Hive ransomware operations, which prosecutors say caused more than $100 million in losses at hospitals, law firms and government bodies. *Why it matters:* The sentence is a rare case of a ransomware operator facing years in a US prison rather than staying out of reach in Russia or Ukraine.
([Justice Department](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/ukrainian-national-sentenced-role-hacking-hospitals-businesses-and-governments-part), [The Record](https://therecord.media/nefilim-hive-ransomware-operator-70-months-prison))

## US military and intelligence community

**The US Army is looking at high-altitude balloons to extend surveillance and strike reach against China.** Defense News reports soldiers are reviving interest in high-flying balloons to help see and strike along the first island chain near China. *Why it matters:* Balloons are cheap, persistent sensors that could fill gaps in satellite and drone coverage in a fight where China could contest the airspace and the electromagnetic spectrum.
([Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/industry/techwatch/2026/10/27/us-army-looks-to-high-flying-balloons-to-help-see-strike-along-front-lines-with-china/))

**The Pentagon is exploring buying cyber effects instead of cyber equipment.** Breaking Defense reports the Department of Defense is weighing a model dubbed Cyber Effects-as-a-Service, meant to let it purchase specific outcomes rather than commissioning bespoke tools for each mission. *Why it matters:* A shift from buying capability to buying results could speed up how fast commanders get usable cyber effects, but it also means relying more on contractors for offensive and defensive operations.
([Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/10/how-the-pentagon-wants-to-buy-cyber-outcomes-not-equipment/))

**Repeated port calls are wearing out Navy warships supporting the Iran campaign.** Defense One reports that Diego Garcia is the only safe maintenance stop for ships operating against Iran, and the remote atoll cannot provide everything the fleet needs. *Why it matters:* A fleet with nowhere nearby to repair and resupply degrades faster, and that strain compounds the longer the Iran campaign continues.
([Defense One](https://www.defenseone.com/threats/2026/10/port-call-situation-wearing-out-navy-warships/416859/))

**The Navy recorded its highest number of serious aviation and ship mishaps in a decade.** USNI News reports Fiscal Year 2026 data show 16 Class A aviation mishaps, the most in at least ten years, across manned, unmanned and ground aviation incidents. *Why it matters:* A rising mishap rate points to strain on training, maintenance or both, at a time the Navy is sustaining high operational tempo over Iran.
([USNI News](https://news.usni.org/2026/10/27/navy-sees-10-year-high-for-class-a-aviation-afloat-mishaps))

## China, Russia, Iran and North Korea

**China has tripled its satellite fleet since 2018, the Defense Intelligence Agency reports.** The DIA's third unclassified report on space threats documents the expansion and includes a previously unreleased image of China's secretive Shenlong spaceplane. *Why it matters:* A satellite fleet that size gives China far more persistent surveillance, communications and potential anti-satellite reach than it had even a few years ago.
([Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/10/dia-china-has-tripled-its-satellite-fleet-since-2018-as-expansion-continues/))

**US intelligence has confirmed design details of China's secret Shenlong spaceplane, and they closely resemble the US X-37B.** The War Zone reports the uncrewed spaceplane's design is strikingly similar to the Space Force's own reusable spaceplane. *Why it matters:* A Chinese spaceplane built on the same template as a US military platform suggests Beijing is matching, not just tracking, a capability Washington has tried to keep ahead on.
([The War Zone](https://www.twz.com/news-features/u-s-intel-confirms-this-is-chinas-secret-spaceplane))

**Satellite data reviewed by a private intelligence firm ties Russian satellites to recent Iran-backed strikes on Saudi Arabia.** According to the Associated Press, Geneva-based AURORAH Geosignal Intelligence found Russian government and military satellites massing over Saudi targets, including East-West pipeline pumping stations and an air base, in the days before and after Houthi and Iraqi militia strikes between July and October; the firm's founder called the correlation a "100% match," and a senior Houthi leader said Iranian advisers pass the group targeting data based on Russian satellite imagery. Russian officials did not respond to the findings. *Why it matters:* If accurate, it shows Russia directly enabling Iran-aligned attacks on Saudi Arabia, not just supplying Iran separately, though the claim rests on one firm's analysis and an adversary-aligned source.
([Washington Times, via AP](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/oct/27/russian-satellites-helping-irans-allies-strike-saudi-arabia-data/))

**Iran International reports Iran's intelligence service engineered an oil minister's ouster to protect a sanctioned oil-sales network.** The outlet, which is based outside Iran and opposes its government, reports that the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) Intelligence Organization moved to preserve a corruption-linked oil-sales operation by shifting its financial routing from the United Arab Emirates to Russia, and that oil minister Mohsen Paknejad resigned hours after a report on a $1.5 billion transfer through Russia's banking system. Iranian officials have not responded to the allegations. *Why it matters:* If accurate, it shows an intelligence service, not the civilian government, now controlling how Iran evades oil sanctions.
([Iran International](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202610060772))

**Ukraine says Belarus has resumed relaying Russian drones toward NATO airspace.** Jamestown Foundation reports the allegation follows the first known case of a Russian military drone being shot down over Lithuanian territory, by Italian Eurofighters flying NATO's air-policing mission in September. *Why it matters:* A drone crossing from Belarus into Lithuanian airspace, even if accidental, tests how NATO responds to incursions along its eastern flank.
([Jamestown Foundation](https://jamestown.org/ukraine-alleges-belarus-resumed-relaying-russian-drones/))

## Germany and Israel

**Bundeswehr medical personnel are training in Japan as part of the multinational Keen Sword exercise.** About 100 German military medical personnel are taking part in Keen Sword 2026 from October 19 to 29, practicing medical evacuation by A400M transport aircraft from Okinawa and follow-on treatment in Tokyo, alongside Japanese, US and other partner forces. *Why it matters:* German participation in a US-Japan exercise focused on the Indo-Pacific is a small sign of Berlin's widening military engagement beyond Europe.
([Bundeswehr, in German](https://www.bundeswehr.de/de/organisation/unterstuetzungsbereich/sanitaetsdienst/keen-sword-2026-japan))
