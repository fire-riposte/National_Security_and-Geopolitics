## Espionage and counterintelligence

**Chinese intelligence allegedly targeted someone close to Nvidia's CEO, and Trump did nothing about it.** US officials identified a Chinese effort to recruit or place someone with direct access to Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, aiming to gain insight into the company's prized AI chips, the New York Times reported. Trump was briefed on the plot in 2025 but took no action, according to the Times. *Why it matters:* It shows the fight over advanced chips with China has moved beyond export controls into direct espionage against US tech executives. ([NYT](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/29/us/politics/nvidia-china-jensen-huang-trump.html?unlocked_article_code=1.MVE.UZd_.QTr6aY67rpwr&smid=url-share), [Cryptobriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/nvidia-ceo-huang-chinese-espionage-plot/))

**A China-linked hacking group quietly rebuilt its network after a US crackdown.** Researchers at Black Lotus Labs found that Volt Typhoon, the Chinese state-linked group that has infiltrated US critical infrastructure, built a new covert network called "Silent Train" using rapidly rotating compromised devices to hide its tracks, CyberScoop reported. The group has used similar tactics to burrow into hundreds of networks tied to power, water and other critical systems. *Why it matters:* It shows Volt Typhoon adapted rather than retreated after its earlier infrastructure was dismantled, keeping a foothold for disruption in a future crisis. ([CyberScoop](https://cyberscoop.com/volt-typhoon-silent-train-covert-ephemeral-network-black-lotus-labs/))

**Hacktivists leaked personal data on more than 41,000 Department of Homeland Security employees.** A group calling itself "Department of Peace" published data on more than 41,000 Department of Homeland Security staff, the BBC and CyberScoop reported. The data was shared with a Swiss hacker-journalist and posted by the transparency site DDoSecrets. *Why it matters:* A breach this size on frontline immigration and security staff creates a lasting targeting risk for the employees exposed. ([BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq8jzkpjr28vo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss), [CyberScoop](https://cyberscoop.com/dhs-employee-data-leak-ddosecrets/))

## US military and intelligence community

**The Pentagon says the Iran war has cost more than $47 billion.** The US military has spent over $47 billion so far on operations against Iran, more than 25% above the Defense Department's July estimate, according to Pentagon data reported by Defense News. *Why it matters:* The growing bill adds pressure on an administration weighing whether to strike Iran again after the midterms. ([Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/pentagon/2026/10/28/us-costs-of-iran-war-rise-to-over-47-billion-according-to-pentagon-estimate/))

**The military is bracing for adversaries to exploit its own surveillance networks.** DefenseScoop reported that US military officials are working to shore up operational security as frontier AI and other technologies make it easier for adversaries to find and exploit vulnerabilities in the military's interconnected sensor and surveillance networks. *Why it matters:* The same automated-surveillance tools that give the US an edge can be turned against it if left exposed. ([DefenseScoop](https://defensescoop.com/2026/10/28/us-military-opsec-risks-automated-surveillance-networks/))

**Congress got an updated accounting of US strikes on Iran's missile and nuclear programs.** The Congressional Research Service delivered a report to Congress, published by USNI News, detailing US and Israeli military operations against Iran since they began on February 28, 2026, including the scope of strikes on Iran's missile and nuclear infrastructure. *Why it matters:* The report gives lawmakers a baseline assessment just as the administration weighs a new round of strikes. ([USNI News](https://news.usni.org/2026/10/28/report-to-congress-on-u-s-military-operations-against-irans-missile-nuclear-programs-2))

## China, Russia, Iran and North Korea

**China's Communist Party expelled 33 officials, including a former Politburo member and a senior general, at its Fifth Plenum.** The purge, confirmed at the party's Fifth Plenum this week, removed former Politburo member Ma Xingrui, former general Zhang Youxia, former senior diplomat Liu Jianchao, and several other former ministers and regulators, according to Investing.com and The Standard. *Why it matters:* The scale of the purge, on top of September's removal of two top generals, shows Xi Jinping still sees disloyalty and corruption as unresolved problems at the top of the party and military. ([Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/economy-news/china-expels-33-officials-including-top-military-leaders-93CH-4982483), [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/china/article/346925/Chinas-Communist-Party-confirms-expulsion-of-33-officials))

**US intelligence warns China could field space-based laser weapons against satellites within a few years.** The Defense Intelligence Agency assessed that China's orbital directed-energy weapons could threaten satellites by the end of the decade, The War Zone reported, as Beijing's space-warfare capabilities advance alongside sightings of its secretive Shenlong spaceplane. *Why it matters:* A credible Chinese capability to blind or disable satellites would put US surveillance, communications and precision-guided weapons at risk in any Taiwan contingency. ([The War Zone](https://www.twz.com/space/u-s-intel-warns-chinas-space-based-directed-energy-weapons-could-soon-threaten-satellites))

**The US is quietly preparing allies for more Iran strikes after the midterms, even as Vance tried to open a line to Tehran.** The US has urged Gulf allies to prepare for Iranian retaliation against energy facilities after the midterm elections, diplomats told Haaretz, while a separate Haaretz report said Vice President JD Vance sent a letter to Iran's National Security Council chief, at Trump's request, to explore a basis for talks. *Why it matters:* The two-track approach, quiet diplomacy alongside strike planning, suggests Washington hasn't settled whether to escalate or negotiate. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/middle-east-news/2026-10-29/ty-article/u-s-warns-mideast-allies-over-possible-iran-strikes-after-midterms-sources-say/000001a1-8650-d9b5-a3b5-cef078d60000), [Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/middle-east-news/2026-10-29/ty-article/report-vance-contacted-iranian-official-at-trumps-behest-to-revive-talks/000001a1-861b-d1b0-a1bb-e63fbec80000))

**Trump's diesel deal with Russia is giving Moscow cash and leverage, experts say.** Defense News reported that the Trump administration's agreement to buy Russian diesel benefits Moscow more than US consumers, according to experts and lawmakers, even as Ukraine is pressed to give up leverage of its own in ceasefire talks. *Why it matters:* Critics say the deal undercuts sanctions pressure on Russia just as the administration says it wants Moscow to negotiate. ([Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/global/europe/2026/10/28/trumps-russian-diesel-deal-benefits-moscow-more-than-us-consumers-experts-say/))

## Germany and Israel

**Israel's election chief asked the Shin Bet to investigate alleged Qatari and Turkish funding meant to boost Arab voter turnout.** Haaretz reported that Israel's election committee chairman asked the Shin Bet to probe claims that foreign funding, allegedly from Qatar, was used to influence turnout among Arab voters, after allies of Prime Minister Netanyahu pushed election-fraud claims following a disputed vote. A senior police official told Haaretz the claims so far appear "baseless." *Why it matters:* Unproven foreign-interference claims, raised in a contested election, are already being used by Likud to pressure the opposition over the Knesset speakership. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/elections/2026-10-29/ty-article/.premium/election-chief-urges-shin-bet-probe-into-alleged-foreign-funding/000001a1-8691-d0bb-a3a9-ded971da0000))

**Germany's governing coalition is fighting over Chancellor Merz's push to rearm, including a possible return of military service.** Defense Minister Boris Pistorius wants a new military service law in effect by January, but the Social Democrats are resisting the mandatory recruitment model pushed by parts of Merz's Christian Democrats and Bavaria's Christian Social Union. Merz separately wants the Bundeswehr to become the strongest conventional army in Europe and has pushed NATO allies toward spending 3.5% of GDP on defense. *Why it matters:* The dispute over mandatory service will decide how fast Germany can actually build the force it says it needs against Russia. ([Yahoo News](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/german-defence-minister-unmoved-military-095432411.html))
