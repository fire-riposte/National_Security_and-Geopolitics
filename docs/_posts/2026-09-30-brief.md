## Espionage and counterintelligence

**Russian hackers Star Blizzard broaden their espionage campaign to reach Ukraine and beyond.** Microsoft says the Kremlin-linked Star Blizzard group has shifted tactics, using bulk phishing that needs only one victim to click, to expand its espionage campaign against US and UK targets and push further into Ukraine-linked networks. *Why it matters:* It shows a veteran Russian intelligence cyber unit adapting its tradecraft rather than losing effectiveness after years of Western exposure and sanctions. ([CyberScoop](https://cyberscoop.com/microsoft-star-blizzard-redflick-phishing-campaigns/))

**Bulgarians held over Munich arson were part of a GRU-linked network run from Slovakia.** Investigators said the group behind an arson attack in Munich was tied to Russia's military intelligence agency, the GRU, and operated out of Slovakia as part of a wider sabotage network. *Why it matters:* It's another data point in the run of GRU-linked sabotage cells operating against Ukraine's European backers, recruited and run from outside the country where they strike. ([The Insider](https://theins.press/en/news/297540))

**German prosecutors charge a Ukrainian national with spying on a Ukrainian veteran for Russia.** Düsseldorf's general prosecutor charged a 53-year-old Ukrainian man from Attendorn with working for a Russian intelligence service since November 2025, allegedly gathering information on a man in Germany who had fought for Ukraine's armed forces; he was arrested in March, and prosecutors say the surveillance likely served to prepare further intelligence operations against the target. *Why it matters:* Russian services are using access to Ukrainian communities abroad to track down and potentially target people who fought against Russia. ([Siegener Zeitung, in German](https://www.siegener-zeitung.de/lokales/kreis-olpe/attendorn/attendorn-anklage-gegen-mutmasslichen-russland-spion-ukraine-kaempfer-ausgespaeht-Q4K4TNXDEVHSZMQFBUFY7CVNL4.html))

**Australian foreign-interference charge dropped against one of three alleged Chinese spies.** Prosecutors withdrew the reckless foreign-interference charge against a 26-year-old man accused of covertly collecting information on a Canberra Buddhist association for a foreign principal; a magistrate granted the withdrawal but awarded the defendant costs and pressed prosecutors to speed up the case against the two women still charged. *Why it matters:* A stumbling prosecution in a high-profile foreign-interference case raises questions about Australia's ability to make these charges stick. ([ABC News Australia](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-30/charges-against-alleged-chinese-spy-dropped/107211166))

## US military and intelligence community

**Dutch police arrest alleged leader of the group that hacked the FBI.** Police in the Netherlands arrested a 24-year-old man in Amsterdam a week before ShinyHunters, the extortion group he's accused of leading, claimed to have stolen 2 to 3 terabytes of FBI personnel data. *Why it matters:* It's the first real law-enforcement result against the group behind a breach that could expose FBI staff working sensitive counterintelligence cases. ([CyberScoop](https://cyberscoop.com/shinyhunters-alleged-leader-arrested-netherlands/))

**Boeing wins Navy's next-generation F/A-XX fighter contract, worth more than $20 billion.** The Pentagon selected Boeing to build the Navy's sixth-generation, carrier-based F/A-XX strike fighter, covering full-scale development and multiple test aircraft, after the program was previously frozen. *Why it matters:* The award gives Boeing a monopoly on America's next generation of stealth fighters, following its earlier selection to build the Air Force's F-47. ([USNI News](https://news.usni.org/2026/09/29/boeing-picked-to-build-navys-f-a-xx-carrier-fighter-in-20b-contract), [Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/industry/techwatch/2026/09/29/us-navy-selects-boeing-to-build-next-generation-fa-xx-fighter/))

**Pentagon's strategy update to Congress skips over gaps it can't easily fix.** Defense One reported the Pentagon's update on the National Defense Strategy touts progress to Congress but leaves out depleted weapons stockpiles, strategic distractions and a quiet pullback from European defense commitments. *Why it matters:* A strategy report that omits its own weak points makes it harder for Congress to weigh whether the Pentagon's posture matches the threats it says it's tracking. ([Defense One](https://www.defenseone.com/threats/2026/09/pentagon-update-national-defense-strategy-cites-progress-omits-key-threats/416329/))

## China, Russia, Iran and North Korea

**CIA director reportedly told Moscow its economy risks Soviet-style collapse.** German outlet Frankfurter Allgemeine Zeitung reported that CIA Director John Ratcliffe warned Russian officials during a late-August Moscow visit that Russia's war-strained economy could collapse "just as it did in the Soviet Union," even as US intelligence assesses the battlefield is heading toward stalemate. *Why it matters:* A warning delivered directly in Moscow suggests Washington is trying to use economic pressure, not just battlefield outcomes, to push Russia toward ending the war. ([United24 Media](https://united24media.com/world/cia-chief-reportedly-warned-russia-its-economy-could-collapse-like-the-soviet-union-22964))

**Netanyahu shows UAE leader new intelligence that Iran is rebuilding its nuclear program.** Netanyahu presented Emirati President Mohammed bin Zayed with intelligence on new construction at a mountain site south of Tehran during a visit Sunday, also attended by a senior Saudi official, and said Israel assesses Iran could strike in the region in the coming weeks. *Why it matters:* Israel is using its intelligence on Iran to shore up a regional coalition with Gulf states even as it warns of a renewed Iranian threat. ([The Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/article-910154), [CNN](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/09/28/middleeast/israel-netanyahu-secret-visit-uae-intl))

**Bipartisan lawmakers push Trump to hold the line on Russia sanctions.** Four House members from both parties, responding to reports the administration is weighing sanctions relief in exchange for Russia releasing political prisoners, urged Trump to instead enforce the Graham Act, which he signed September 19 and which requires him to announce sanctions on Russia's top oil buyers by October 18. *Why it matters:* Congress is signaling it won't accept a quiet rollback of sanctions pressure even as the administration explores diplomatic openings with Moscow. ([Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/trump-faces-bipartisan-pushback-over-possible-russia-sanctions-relief/), [Ukrainska Pravda](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2026/09/30/8055741/))

**Last US forces leave Iraq after two decades, a move Iran calls a win.** US forces were set to depart their remaining bases in Iraq by Wednesday, ending a two-decade presence that Iran and its allies are celebrating as a victory. *Why it matters:* The withdrawal removes a US check on Iranian influence in Iraq just as Washington and Tehran remain at odds over the war and sanctions. ([Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/news/your-military/2026/09/29/us-forces-exiting-iraq-after-2-decades-leaving-opening-for-iran/))

## Germany and Israel

**Shin Bet in a fight over protecting Netanyahu's rivals.** Israel's Shin Bet said it won't provide a security detail for former IDF chief Gadi Eisenkot despite an advisory committee's recommendation, saying it will instead protect all party leaders through other measures including armed private guards, as Israel's High Court separately weighs a petition to order Shin Bet protection for opposition leader Yair Golan. *Why it matters:* A dispute over protecting opposition politicians is playing out just as Israel heads toward elections. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/israel-security/2026-09-29/ty-article/.premium/despite-official-recommendation-shin-bet-wont-protect-top-netanyahu-challenger/000001a0-ee04-d272-adf6-ffc60be30000))

**IDF didn't tell police or Shin Bet before a West Bank operation that turned into a riot.** Police and Shin Bet sources said the army didn't coordinate in advance when it moved to return a displaced Palestinian family to their home in Jalud, leaving only a dozen Border Police officers on call for backup when more than 100 settlers rioted, attacked the family and troops, and set fires; a small IDF force sheltered with the family until reinforcements arrived. *Why it matters:* A coordination failure inside Israel's own security establishment left a small unit exposed and let a riot spiral before backup could respond. ([The Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/army-reportedly-failed-to-coordinate-jalud-return-with-police-shin-bet-leaving-soldiers-outmanned))
