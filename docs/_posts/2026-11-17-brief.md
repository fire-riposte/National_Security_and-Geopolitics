**Bottom line:** Russia's hybrid campaign against Europe kept escalating: Estonia detained eight suspects over a sabotage attempt it blames on the GRU, and Ukraine says it brought down the Kerch Bridge's road span in an unprecedented strike on Crimea. In the Gulf, Iran claims it hit a US radar site in Bahrain and US officials are tracking reports that Tehran is weighing a dirty bomb or chemical attack in Washington, even as the nine-month war stays in diplomatic limbo. In Washington, the Army officer accused of spying for Russia from a NATO base in England is expected to be charged within days, and the Jensen Huang spy saga keeps reshaping the debate over China's access to US chips.

## Espionage and counterintelligence

**US Army officer set to be charged over alleged Russia spying, case headed to military court.** UK officials said Lt. Col. Brandon Tyner, the Army human-resources officer arrested near Gloucester, England, is expected to be formally charged by British prosecutors under the National Security Act, with a detention warrant holding him until November 19. US officials said that once Britain files charges, the case will move to a court-martial under US military law; a US official said no protected information is believed to have reached Moscow, and the Kremlin has denied any link to Tyner. *Why it matters:* a formal UK charge would be the first concrete legal step in a case that already exposed an alleged Russian recruitment effort against an officer at a NATO headquarters. ([Arab News](https://www.arabnews.pk/world/us-officer-accused-of-russian-spying-is-set-to-be-charged-in-uk-official-says-3006895), [IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.com/us-army-officer-arrested-britain-russia-spying-suspicion-3809749))

**Trump defends Nvidia's Huang as "Chinese spy" scandal keeps evolving.** President Donald Trump said he spoke with Jensen Huang, who resigned as Nvidia's chief executive on November 12 amid the fallout, and said he believes Huang's account that he didn't know the Chinese businesswoman he was involved with, Alice Huang, was allegedly a Chinese intelligence asset. Trump said Xi Jinping could raise the case when the two leaders meet. Separately, US authorities are examining Megaspeed, the Singapore-based chip buyer Alice Huang once worked for, over possible ties to Chinese intelligence; Beijing's foreign ministry has called the spying allegations groundless. *Why it matters:* a personal scandal around one of the most important US chip executives is now shaping both Nvidia's leadership and Washington's debate over Chinese access to advanced chips. ([South China Morning Post](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/diplomacy/article/3373857/trump-backs-nvidias-huang-over-spy-allegations-says-xi-may-raise-case), [IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/nvidia-investigates-possible-chinese-intelligence-operation-1830951))

**Google says Russian-speaking hackers ran the first fully automated AI cyberattack.** Google's Threat Intelligence Group said Russian-speaking hackers carried out what it describes as the first fully automated cyberattack, with AI agents handling reconnaissance and intrusion with little human direction. The tools traded stealth for speed, leaving noisy forensic traces even as they succeeded on their own. *Why it matters:* autonomous attack tools are starting to outpace the manual, human-paced intrusions that defenders are built to catch. ([CyberScoop](https://cyberscoop.com/google-fully-automated-ai-cyberattack/))

**King Charles opens new headquarters for Britain's offensive hacking unit.** King Charles opened a new headquarters near Samlesbury in northwest England for the National Cyber Force, the secretive unit that runs Britain's offensive cyber operations. *Why it matters:* Britain is giving its offensive hacking arm a permanent public home, a sign of how central offensive cyber has become to UK security planning. ([The Record](https://therecord.media/uk-national-cyber-force-headquarters-opens))

## US military and intelligence community

**Senate appropriators unveil $1.1 trillion Pentagon spending bill.** Senate appropriators released a $1.1 trillion Pentagon spending bill that folds in nearly $69 billion the White House had sought through a separate reconciliation package into the base budget. *Why it matters:* Congress is consolidating defense spending into the regular appropriations process rather than relying on the reconciliation money the Pentagon had been counting on. ([Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/11/senate-appropriators-unveil-1-1t-pentagon-spending-bill-folding-in-reconciliation-priorities/))

**NSA warns of malware hijacking computers through military ID card readers.** The National Security Agency warned that new malware is hijacking personal computers when users insert their Common Access Cards, the military's identification smart cards, into connected card readers. *Why it matters:* a compromised card reader can expose the credentials service members and contractors use to reach military networks. ([Department of War](https://www.war.gov/News/News-Stories/Article/Article/4649563/nsa-warns-about-potential-malware-hijacking-cac-enabled-computers/))

**Army orders "urgent" build of concrete bunkers across Middle East bases.** The Army said in a notice that US soldiers in the Middle East are "currently at risk" in personnel shelters made from converted shipping containers, and ordered urgent construction of concrete bunkers for troops in Israel, Saudi Arabia and Lebanon. *Why it matters:* the Pentagon is acknowledging, under pressure from the still-unresolved Iran conflict, that current force protection in the region isn't adequate. ([Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/global/mideast-africa/2026/11/16/us-army-takes-urgent-action-to-build-concrete-bunkers-across-the-middle-east/), [The War Zone](https://www.twz.com/news-features/u-s-army-now-wants-concrete-bunkers-for-troops-in-israel-saudi-arabia-and-lebanon))

## China, Russia, Iran and North Korea

**Iran claims drone strike on US radar in Bahrain; US disputes damage.** Iran's Revolutionary Guard said it launched six drones at a US-linked air-defense radar at a helicopter base in Bahrain, calling it retaliation for "hostile US actions." Bahrain said it intercepted multiple Iranian aerial attacks and issued its first threat alerts since September; US Central Command rejected Iran's claim that the radar was destroyed and reported no injuries or damage. *Why it matters:* Iran is still testing US and Gulf defenses even as diplomacy toward ending the nine-month war has produced no breakthrough. ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/15/iran-says-it-targets-us-radar-in-bahrain-what-do-we-know))

**Iran developing plans for dirty bomb or chemical attack in Washington, CNN reports.** CNN reported, citing US officials, that Iran has been developing plans for a possible dirty bomb or chemical attack in Washington, though officials said there is no evidence Tehran has decided to carry one out. The White House said there are no credible threats to US territory; Iran denied the allegations. *Why it matters:* even an unexecuted plan shows US intelligence is treating Iran's capability and intent as a live concern rather than a past-tense threat. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/us-news/2026-11-17/ty-article/iran-developing-plans-for-dirty-bomb-or-chemical-attack-in-d-c-report-says/000001a1-e8c8-d848-abeb-fac893430000))

**Ukraine destroys Kerch Bridge road span in strike on Crimea.** Ukraine said it brought down a section of the Kerch Bridge's road span connecting occupied Crimea to Russia, with President Volodymyr Zelensky calling the strike "unprecedented." The Kremlin vowed to rebuild the crossing. *Why it matters:* Ukraine has now struck Russia's most symbolically important link to Crimea in back-to-back days, widening the war's reach into a target Moscow long treated as untouchable. ([BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cw772jk4rvmxo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss), [The War Zone](https://www.twz.com/news-features/kerch-bridge-struck-for-the-second-time-in-less-than-24-hours-russia))

**Eight detained after Russia-linked sabotage attempt at Estonian drone maker.** Estonian authorities detained eight suspects, reportedly including Latvian nationals, over a failed arson attack at Threod Systems, a drone maker near Tallinn whose equipment has been used in Ukraine; Estonian officials say Russia's military intelligence agency, the GRU, ordered the attempt, which the company's own security staff stopped before any damage occurred. *Why it matters:* Russia's campaign against European defense manufacturers is now generating arrests and direct GRU attribution serious enough that Estonia is closing its border crossings with Russia in response. ([Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/11/15/estonian-drone-maker-targeted-by-gru-ordered-sabotage-attempt-pm-says))

## Germany and Israel

**Shin Bet questioned dozens in election probe despite lacking evidence, sources say.** Haaretz reported that Shin Bet questioned dozens of people in its investigation into irregularities in the recent election despite lacking evidence, deploying wiretaps and advanced surveillance tools; agency chief David Zini accused the attorney general of tying the service's hands, though the election committee chief said she had not rejected the agency's investigation requests. *Why it matters:* Israel's domestic security service is pushing the boundaries of an open-ended election probe even as its own oversight structure disputes whether it has grounds to proceed. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/elections/2026-11-16/ty-article/.premium/shin-bet-to-question-dozens-in-election-probe-despite-lack-of-evidence/000001a1-e557-d462-abf7-ffff7c000000))

**Bundeswehr personnel tops 190,000, exceeding 2026 growth target early.** Germany's defense ministry said the Bundeswehr has grown to roughly 190,000 active soldiers, its highest level in 13 years, exceeding the legally mandated 2026 growth corridor of 186,000 to 190,000 ahead of schedule after a surge in October enlistments; applications are up 29 percent this year. *Why it matters:* Berlin's recruitment drive, launched to rebuild the Bundeswehr without reinstating conscription, is outperforming its own targets. ([militaeraktuell.at](https://militaeraktuell.at/en/personnel-target-exceeded-the-bundeswehr-is-growing-faster-than-planned/))
