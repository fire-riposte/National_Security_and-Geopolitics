**Bottom line:** Estonia is closing its entire border with Russia after accusing Russian military intelligence of a sabotage attempt on a drone maker, the latest hybrid attack Tallinn has blamed on Moscow. In Washington, fallout from the Nvidia-Megaspeed China spying case is pushing Congress toward tighter AI chip export controls, while in the Middle East, Mossad's chief met the CIA director and Trump's envoy on Iran as Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu keeps pressing for a strike that Washington, Israel's military (the IDF) and his own political opposition are all blocking.

## Espionage and counterintelligence

**Army names officer held in Russia espionage probe.** The Army identified the human resources officer arrested last week in Gloucestershire, England, on suspicion of helping Russian intelligence as Lt. Col. Brandon Carroll Tyner, 41, who was serving at Imjin Barracks, home to NATO's Allied Rapid Reaction Corps. He remains in custody under Britain's National Security Act while British counterterrorism police, the FBI and US Army Counterintelligence Command continue investigating two locations in Gloucestershire; no charges have been filed. *Why it matters:* a serving US officer accused of spying for Russia from inside a NATO headquarters is a serious counterintelligence breach regardless of his rank. ([Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/global/europe/2026/11/13/us-army-names-officer-held-on-allegations-of-espionage/))

**Israeli man indicted for spying for Iran out of anger over the Gaza war.** Prosecutors allege the man contacted Iranian intelligence directly and passed on information about military vehicle movements and a photo of a drone defense system deployed above a police station, reportedly seeking no payment. *Why it matters:* an Israeli citizen volunteering to spy for Tehran for free shows Iran can still find recruits driven by grievance rather than money. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/middle-east-news/iran/2026-11-15/ty-article/.premium/israeli-man-indicted-for-spying-for-iran-driven-by-anger-over-gaza-war/000001a1-def9-dbd4-adb1-fef933c20000))

**Congress pushes AI chip export curbs as Nvidia-Megaspeed case details emerge.** Singapore-based Megaspeed said Alice Huang, the woman at the center of espionage allegations tied to Nvidia's recently departed CEO Jensen Huang, left the company in 2025; the case has raised questions about whether advanced Nvidia chips reached China despite US export limits. Senator Pete Ricketts has cited the Nvidia-Megaspeed case in pushing his Match Act, which the Senate's draft defense bill now includes alongside the Chip Security Act and the AI Overwatch Act. *Why it matters:* a single alleged economic-espionage case is now shaping how Congress writes AI chip export law. ([BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmdx1vpprk9vo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss), [Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/world/20261111/us-lawmakers-push-for-tougher-china-export-controls-in-defense-bill-amid-nvidia-scrutiny))

**FBI warns an AI tool is letting hackers move at "machine speed."** The FBI's flash alert said the open-source tool Artex "significantly reduces the time required to chain attack techniques," with some intrusions reaching privileged access in under three hours. Microsoft has tied Artex to Storm-0940, a China state-aligned group that used it after compromising ConnectWise's cloud backup service, attempting sign-ins to more than 2,250 Microsoft 365 tenants and gaining administrator-level access in some cases. *Why it matters:* AI-assisted tooling is cutting the time defenders have to catch a state-backed intrusion before it escalates. ([The Record](https://therecord.media/artex-ai-tool-fbi-flash-alert), [CSO Online](https://www.csoonline.com/article/4239028/china-linked-hackers-used-agentic-ai-in-attack-on-2250-microsoft-365-tenants.html))

**Mossad reportedly abducted Iranian generals in a flawed hunt for a missing airman.** A New York Times investigation, reported by Haaretz, found that Mossad abducted two Iranian generals in an effort to learn the fate of airman Ron Arad, missing since he disappeared over Lebanon in 1986; other accounts describe the operation as unsuccessful. *Why it matters:* the claim rests on foreign reporting rather than confirmation from Israeli officials, who operate under military censorship on covert operations, but it shows how far Israeli intelligence has gone chasing a decades-old case. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/middle-east-news/iran/2026-11-15/ty-article/report-israel-abducted-iranian-generals-in-secret-quest-for-missing-airman/000001a1-df0f-d1ce-a9a3-ff1fd6c90000))

## US military and intelligence community

**Trump's $1.45 trillion defense budget request is effectively dead.** War on the Rocks reports the 2026 midterms likely dealt a final blow to the Pentagon's prospective $1.45 trillion Fiscal Year 2027 budget, though a path remains to salvage the base $1.1 trillion request. *Why it matters:* a change in Congress's makeup after the midterms, not Pentagon planning, is now driving defense spending decisions. ([War on the Rocks](https://warontherocks.com/cogs-of-war/rip-to-the-1-45-trillion-fy2027-defense-budget/))

**Suspected drones trigger security responses at US bases in Britain.** The US Air Force evacuated aircraft and raised the threat level at RAF Mildenhall over suspected drone activity, days after Iran reportedly threatened British air bases; the director of Britain's domestic security service, MI5, said an Iranian attempt to hit a British base would "not be unique." A separate suspected-drone incident triggered a security response at RAF Lakenheath. *Why it matters:* repeated, unexplained drone incursions near US aircraft in Britain raise the stakes of each incident regardless of who turns out to be behind it. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/us-news/2026-11-13/ty-article/drone-activity-reported-at-u-s-base-in-britain-days-after-iran-threat/000001a1-d6b1-d1ce-a9a3-f6b367810000), [The War Zone](https://www.twz.com/air/suspected-drones-over-raf-lakenheath-just-triggered-security-response-u-s-air-force))

**US forces down Iranian drones as Iran claims further strikes.** US Central Command (CENTCOM) said it shot down two Iranian attack drones with no injuries or damage after Iran said it struck a US radar site in Bahrain; Bahrain later said it intercepted additional Iranian drone attacks, which Iran's Revolutionary Guard called retaliation for "hostile US actions." *Why it matters:* even with Trump calling the war with Iran "effectively over," US forces in the Gulf are still absorbing and repelling Iranian strikes. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/middle-east-news/iran/2026-11-13/ty-article/u-s-downs-two-iranian-drones-as-iran-says-it-struck-u-s-radar-in-bahrain/000001a1-d63d-d1ce-a9a3-f63f97d80000), [Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/middle-east-news/2026-11-15/ty-article/bahrain-intercepts-iranian-drone-attacks-as-tehran-says-it-hit-u-s-base/000001a1-debc-da30-a7fb-debdb7ed0000))

## China, Russia, Iran and North Korea

**Estonia to shut its entire border with Russia after accusing the GRU of sabotage.** Estonian Prime Minister Kristen Michal said an attempted sabotage attack on drone maker Threod Systems was "GRU-ordered," referring to Russian military intelligence, and announced Estonia will close all road border crossings with Russia starting December 1. *Why it matters:* a NATO member is cutting off its border with Russia entirely over an attack on a domestic drone manufacturer, calling it part of Russia's hybrid war on Europe. ([Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/11/estonian-drone-company-targeted-in-sabotage-attempt-as-part-of-russias-hybrid-war-against-europe-prime-minister-michal/))

**Mossad chief meets CIA and Trump's envoy on Iran as Netanyahu pushes for war.** Axios reported, per Haaretz, that Mossad chief David Gofman met the CIA director in Washington and was set to meet Trump's envoy Steve Witkoff, amid reported Israeli efforts to persuade the administration to join an attack on Iran. *Why it matters:* Israel is actively lobbying Washington for renewed military action against Iran even as Trump has called the prior war over. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/middle-east-news/iran/2026-11-13/ty-article/.premium/mossad-chief-meets-u-s-officials-to-discuss-iran-as-netanyahu-pushes-for-war/000001a1-d57c-da30-a7fb-ddfd4a810000))

**Trump reportedly leans against new Iran strikes as the IAEA flags enrichment concerns.** Haaretz reported Trump told advisers he may hold off on further strikes on Iran barring new provocations, with Saudi Arabia and Turkey urging restraint and US intelligence assessing that renewed strikes would do little to change Tehran's position. Separately, the chief of the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) said he remains concerned about enrichment activity at Iran's Isfahan site. *Why it matters:* Washington's own intelligence assessment is a direct check on Netanyahu's push for renewed military action. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/us-news/2026-11-14/ty-article/sources-trump-tells-advisers-he-may-avoid-new-iran-strikes-unless-provoked/000001a1-d9ee-d292-abf9-fdff02d60000), [Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/middle-east-news/iran/2026-11-14/ty-article/nuclear-watchdog-increasingly-concerned-about-iranian-enrichment-plant/000001a1-d68c-d1ce-a9a3-f69f70480000))

**A retired admiral revisits his own warning that China could invade Taiwan by 2027.** The War Zone marks the approach of the "Davidson Window," the 2027 timeframe for a possible Chinese move on Taiwan that Adm. Philip Davidson predicted in 2021, and reports Davidson now hopes his own prediction will prove wrong. *Why it matters:* as the window Davidson named draws near, the US military's own planning assumptions about a Taiwan contingency are getting fresh scrutiny. ([The War Zone](https://www.twz.com/news-features/davidson-window-admiral-hopes-he-was-wrong-on-chinas-ability-to-take-taiwan-by-2027))

## Germany and Israel

**Bundestag gives German spy agencies new hacking and sabotage powers.** The Bundestag passed the bill 308 to 190, with 71 abstentions, in what The Record calls the biggest overhaul of Germany's spy laws since World War II, months after Chancellor Friedrich Merz's cabinet signed off on the draft. *Why it matters:* Germany is substantially expanding what its intelligence agencies can legally do, including offensive cyber and sabotage powers they did not previously have. ([The Record](https://therecord.media/german-parliament-passes-law-giving-spy-agencies-hacking-power))

**Shin Bet chief sought to wiretap Arab lawmakers before the election, Haaretz reports.** Haaretz reports Shin Bet chief David Zini sought to wiretap Arab members of Knesset ahead of the election but failed to give the attorney general evidence to support the request; one official said Zini "has no basic understanding of how things work in a democracy." *Why it matters:* a domestic security service seeking to surveil elected lawmakers without adequate justification is a direct test of Israeli intelligence oversight. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/israel-politics/2026-11-15/ty-article/.premium/shin-bet-chief-sought-to-wiretap-arab-lawmakers-before-the-election/000001a1-e0ee-dfb8-adfd-ecee77f70000))

**German military bans Chinese-made drones from bases.** Germany's defense ministry said Chinese-made drones, including DJI models used for pilot training and as practice targets, will no longer be used and will be replaced with German or other European products, citing data and IT security; Spiegel reported Germany's military counterintelligence service found traces of data leaks on Bundeswehr drones, which the ministry has not confirmed. *Why it matters:* Berlin is repeating a 2023 ban it never fully enforced, showing how hard militaries find it to purge Chinese hardware from their own supply chains. ([Hürriyet Daily News](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/german-military-bans-chinese-made-drones-229271))

**Netanyahu keeps pushing for an Iran strike despite being blocked on all sides.** Haaretz reports Netanyahu continues pushing for an attack on Iran even as the Trump administration, the IDF and his own domestic political opposition all stand in the way of an immediate strike. *Why it matters:* the gap between Netanyahu's stated ambitions on Iran and what his own military and government will authorize keeps widening. ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/israel-security/2026-11-16/ty-article/.premium/netanyahu-blocked-from-all-directions-continues-pushing-for-an-iran-strike/000001a1-e0c6-d292-abf9-fdffc5d70000))
