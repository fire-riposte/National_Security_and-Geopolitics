Each weekday morning an automated Claude Code routine reads about 30 news feeds,
including defense and intelligence outlets, think tanks, US government press
releases, Google News searches and the r/espionage community. It ranks the
stories by relevance to US national security, espionage and great-power
competition, then writes a short brief with a link for every claim.

The briefs are summaries, not original reporting, and they can contain mistakes.
Check the linked sources before relying on anything here.

The source list and editorial rules live in the repository in `feeds.toml` and
`BRIEFING.md`.
